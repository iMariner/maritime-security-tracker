A RECORD WITH ACCOUNTABILITY

What these numbers mean.

Evidence, kept in view

Confirmed, reported and claimed are the existing tracker’s evidence labels. They describe the incident, not automatic confirmation of every detail. Several outlets may repeat the same original account.

People are never an estimate

Death totals use only structured, explicitly confirmed casualty records with supporting links. Narrative casualty reports remain visible, but are not converted into totals. Nationality is never inferred from flag, name or prose.

Scope, with limits

These are merchant-vessel incident records in the three named regions. Merged duplicates, rejected records and satellite-only signals are excluded. Record counts are not separate attack totals: one event can affect several vessels, and the existing matching process can group repeat reports involving the same vessel.

Changes remain readable

Attack dates determine timeline order. The updates section highlights recently changed records, including older incidents. A record’s update timestamp alone does not establish what changed. Explicit revision notes are shown when supplied.
