How we verify

01

Reviewed before counted.

Only named, dated incidents with supporting sources and an explicit evidence review enter this public record. Source checking does not turn a reported claim into official confirmation; accounts remain attributed to their sources.

02

People are never estimates.

Deaths and nationalities require explicit supporting evidence. Nationality is never inferred from a vessel’s flag. An incomplete casualty account is labelled as such. These totals cover seafarers, including maritime pilots; shore-worker deaths are kept separate.

03

A record that can change.

Attacks appear by incident date, newest first. Later evidence updates the same record, with dated revision notes. Several publications may repeat one original report.

04

Our view has boundaries.

We cover merchant vessels in the Black Sea, Red Sea and Strait of Hormuz. Incidents and distinct vessels are counted separately. Vessel drawings are illustrations, not documentary images.
