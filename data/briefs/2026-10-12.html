Tanker Petro Dolphin hit by drone off Oman, two Indian seafarers missing

India's foreign ministry and shipping minister named the Nicaragua-flagged tanker struck at Sohar anchorage on 11 October.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 12 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • India's Ministry of External Affairs named the Nicaragua-flagged tanker Petro Dolphin and its two missing Indian seafarers.
  • UKMTO said the tanker in the Strait of Hormuz was struck by an unidentified projectile and all crew were safe.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden or the Black Sea.

India's Ministry of External Affairs and Shipping Minister Sarbananda Sonowal said the Nicaragua-flagged tanker Petro Dolphin was hit by a drone at Sohar anchorage in Oman on 11 October, leaving two Indian seafarers missing and nine rescued.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (11 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The ministry and the minister named the vessel, its flag and the two Indian seafarers missing with nine rescued after the strike at Sohar anchorage. UKMTO did not name the ship. The attacker has not been identified.

UKMTO, the UK maritime agency, reported that a tanker transiting outbound in the Strait of Hormuz was struck on its port side by an unidentified projectile on 11 October, with damage reported and all crew safe. UKMTO gave no vessel name, flag or position.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden or the Black Sea in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
