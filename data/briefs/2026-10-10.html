Tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO said the vessel was hit by an unknown projectile 13 nautical miles west of Al Jazeera Al Hamra on 9 October, with all 22 Indian crew reported safe.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 10 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • UK maritime agency reported the crude oil tanker hit at 1709 GMT on 6 October in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • UKMTO said the fire on the vessel was extinguished after the strike.
  • Iran's IRGC claimed it struck an LPG tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on 9 October.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden or the Black Sea.

A tanker was struck by an unknown projectile 13 nautical miles west of Al Jazeera Al Hamra in the United Arab Emirates on 9 October, causing a fire that was extinguished, UKMTO said.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 8 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (8 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

A crude oil tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz at 1709 GMT on 6 October, according to a UK maritime agency. No casualties were reported in that delayed account. The Times of India separately said Iran attacked a ship in UAE waters.

UKMTO said a vessel was struck by an unknown projectile 13 nautical miles west of Al Jazeera in the United Arab Emirates on 9 October, causing a fire that was later extinguished. Other outlets described the same strike as the second ship hit inside the Persian Gulf in three days and as part of a wider wave of Iranian attacks, including a Taiwanese-managed VLCC, with no vessel name, flag or attacker confirmed.

Iran's IRGC claimed a strike on an LPG tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on 9 October and threatened to punish vessels trying to cross. A separate headline reported Iran attacking a Vietnamese ship in the Strait of Hormuz the same day, with no vessel name, flag or damage details given.

Earlier in the week, the IMO lists ST1 (IMO 9256999) as hit at 2NM off the coast of Oman, near Jazirat on 6 October 2026.

Earlier in the week, the IMO lists GAS VELA (IMO 9969845) as hit at Strait of Hormuz on 4 October 2026.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden or the Black Sea in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
