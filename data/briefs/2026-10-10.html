Tanker hit by projectile in Strait of Hormuz, 11 Indian crew injured

A UK maritime agency reported the strike on 8 October, after which the UN halted its escort of ships through Hormuz.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 10 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • The UN halted its escort of ships through Hormuz after the 8 October strike.
  • UKMTO said the 9 October projectile hit 13 nautical miles west of Al Jazeera.
  • News reports said 23 Indian crew were rescued from the burning tanker off Sochi.
  • Indian minister Sonowal said all 22 Indian crew at Sharjah anchorage were safe.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

A crude oil tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 8 October, a UK maritime agency said, with 11 Indian crew reported injured.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 9 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (8 the week before); 3 in the Black Sea (0 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The vessel is a Panama-flagged crude oil tanker, according to reports. The UN halted its escort of ships through Hormuz after the attack, and a Times of India headline separately said Iran attacked a ship in UAE waters. No attacker has been confirmed.

UKMTO said a vessel was struck by an unknown projectile 13 nautical miles west of Al Jazeera in the United Arab Emirates on 9 October, causing a fire that was later extinguished. Other outlets described the same strike as a second ship hit inside the Persian Gulf in three days and as part of a wider wave of Iranian attacks, including a Taiwanese-managed VLCC. No vessel name, flag or attacker has been confirmed.

An oil tanker with 22 Indian crew members was attacked at Sharjah anchorage, Indian minister Sonowal said. All crew were reported safe. No attacker has been confirmed.

Iran's IRGC claimed a strike on an LPG tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on 9 October and threatened to punish vessels trying to cross.

Earlier in the week, the IMO lists ST1 (IMO 9256999) as hit at 2NM off the coast of Oman, near Jazirat on 6 October 2026.

Earlier in the week, the IMO lists GAS VELA (IMO 9969845) as hit at Strait of Hormuz on 4 October 2026.

Black Sea

A sea drone attack set a tanker ablaze off Sochi in the Black Sea around 8 to 9 October, according to news reports, and 23 Indian crew members were rescued. Related reports describe a drone attack on a Turkish vessel with 10 crew killed and Indian condemnation of strikes that injured an Indian sailor. No vessel name, flag or position has been given.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
