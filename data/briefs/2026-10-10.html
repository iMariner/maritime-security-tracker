Panama-flagged tanker Gem No. 2 hit by projectile off UAE

Fire aboard the crude oil tanker off Al Jazeera Al Hamra was extinguished and all 22 Indian crew are reported safe.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 10 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Shipping sources named the vessel as the Panama-flagged crude oil tanker Gem No. 2.
  • Iran's IRGC claimed it struck the Vietnam-flagged LPG tanker NV Sunshine in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden or the Black Sea.

UKMTO said the Panama-flagged crude oil tanker Gem No. 2 was struck by an unknown projectile 13 nautical miles west of Al Jazeera Al Hamra in the United Arab Emirates on 9 October, causing a fire that was extinguished.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 8 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (8 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

Shipping sources including gCaptain, Seatrade Maritime and VesselFinder named the vessel as the Panama-flagged crude oil tanker Gem No. 2. UKMTO reported the fire was extinguished and all 22 Indian crew are safe. The attacker has not been confirmed.

Iran's IRGC claimed a strike on the Vietnam-flagged LPG tanker NV Sunshine in the Strait of Hormuz on 9 October, a name given by Hindustan Times and Euronews, and threatened to punish vessels trying to cross. No damage or casualty details have been confirmed.

Earlier in the week, the IMO listed ST1 as hit 2 nautical miles off the coast of Oman, near Jazirat, on 6 October, damaged with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, the IMO listed Gas Vela as hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 4 October, damaged with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, UKMTO reported a crude oil tanker struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz at 1709 GMT on 6 October, with no casualties.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden or the Black Sea in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
