Crew missing after drone strike sets Wine Trader on fire off Romania

Seven of the 12 crew are missing after the San Marino-flagged chemical tanker was hit near Sulina on 15 October, Romanian authorities said.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 17 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Romanian officials said a second tanker was also set on fire in the same area on 15 October.
  • Bulgaria's president named the sunken ship as HS Dignity, correcting early reports that said Azov Concord.
  • Five of the 15 crew rescued from Chelsea 3 were treated for light injuries, Bulgaria's government said.
  • The IMO list records one seafarer injured on DP World Kaveri in the Strait of Hormuz on 11 October.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

Romania's president said a drone struck the San Marino-flagged chemical tanker Wine Trader about 13 nautical miles southeast of Sulina on 15 October, setting it on fire, with seven of 12 crew missing.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 5 in the Black Sea (2 the week before); 9 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (9 the week before).

Black Sea

Five of the 12 crew were rescued, Romanian authorities said. gCaptain and Al Jazeera placed the vessel about one mile outside Romanian territorial waters. Romanian officials said a second tanker was also set on fire in the same area.

Bulgaria's prime minister said the Guinea-Bissau-flagged general cargo ship HS Dignity sank after a drone attack about 17 nautical miles east of Krapets on 13 October. Bulgarian officials said five crew were rescued and 11 were missing. Bulgaria's president named the vessel; early reports naming the Azov Concord were wrong, as that ship took part in the rescue.

Bulgaria's prime minister said the Maltese-flagged, Ukrainian-owned general cargo ship Chelsea 3 was hit by three drones about 18 nautical miles east of Byala early on 13 October. All 15 crew, seven Ukrainians and eight Azerbaijanis, were rescued and five were treated for light injuries, the Bulgarian government said. The ship stayed afloat.

A headline report said Ukraine hit a Russian vessel with a sea drone in the Black Sea on 16 October. No vessel name, flag, position or damage details were given.

Authorities ordered that safe mooring be provided in Novorossiysk for a tanker that had been attacked, according to local reports. The item gave no date, vessel name, flag or details of the attack.

Earlier in the week, romania's president confirmed that drones struck the Comoros-flagged chemical tanker Yara J inside Romanian waters near Sulina late on 14 October. The crew were unharmed and the fire was extinguished, Romania said.

Earlier in the week, russia's defence ministry claimed it struck dry cargo ships carrying military and dual-use cargo, one in the Bug Estuary and one at Chornomorsk, on 15 October. It gave no vessel names, flags or damage details, and Reuters said it could not verify the claim.

Earlier in the week, a Turkish outlet reported drone attacks on seven ships in the Black Sea on 15 October and asked whether NATO's Article 5 could be triggered. No vessel names, flags or positions were given.

Earlier in the week, a headline reported that drones attacked at least seven cargo ships in the Black Sea on 15 October. No vessel names, flags, positions or dates were given.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The IMO confirmed incidents list records DP World Kaveri as hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 11 October, with the vessel damaged, no pollution and one seafarer injured.

The IMO confirmed incidents list records Ever Vict as hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 11 October, with the vessel damaged, no pollution and two seafarers injured.

Iran's navy reported a fire on an oil tanker in the Gulf of Oman on 16 October. No vessel name, flag, position or cause was given.

A tanker near Qatar reported being hit by several projectiles on 16 October, according to a headline report. No vessel name, flag or further details were given.

Earlier in the week, jMIC lists GFS Pride as struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 13 October and sinking. Singapore's Maritime and Port Authority, the flag state, confirmed the sinking with all 21 crew safely evacuated.

Earlier in the week, the IMO confirmed incidents list records GFS Giselle as hit 6 nautical miles off the coast of Oman on 13 October, damaged with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, the IMO confirmed incidents list records Al Saadiyat as hit 1 nautical mile off the coast of Oman on 13 October, damaged with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, the IMO confirmed incidents list records Angola Prosperity as hit 8 nautical miles off the coast of Oman on 11 October, damaged with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, the IMO confirmed incidents list records Al Maqam as hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 11 October, damaged with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, the IMO confirmed incidents list records Kition M as hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 11 October, damaged with limited pollution reported.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.
