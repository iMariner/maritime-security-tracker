Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 1 November 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Turkish outlets said the ship passed the Istanbul Strait after being struck by a drone.
  • Russia's defence ministry said the vessel was carrying goods for Ukraine's armed forces.
  • Iran said the tanker was the fourth to hit a mine in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • The Houthis said they attacked a Saudi tanker in the Red Sea.

A Turkish headline reported on 31 October that a ship struck by a UAV attack in the Black Sea later transited the Istanbul Strait, without naming the vessel or giving its flag, position, damage or casualties.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 1 in the Black Sea (1 the week before); 3 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (4 the week before).

Black Sea

Haber Olsun, Haber61, Habererk.com and Haberler.com carried the headline. No date for the attack, position or casualty details were given, and the report did not say who launched the drone.

Russia's defence ministry claimed a dry cargo vessel carrying goods for Ukraine's armed forces was struck while under way 18 km south of Odesa on the evening of 31 October, according to ASTRA and RIA Novosti. The same ministry roundup said its forces struck maritime vessels used in the interests of Ukraine's armed forces. No vessel name, position, damage or casualty details were given.

Earlier in the week, sea drones struck the container ship Contship Jet off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast on 28 October while she was en route to Constanța, Romania, according to the owner and the Bulgarian government, with the crew reported safe.

Earlier in the week, a Greek container ship sank after a drone attack off Bulgaria, according to a headline.

Earlier in the week, russia's defence ministry claimed its forces struck a dry cargo vessel in the Black Sea carrying military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa as part of a massed overnight strike with drones and precision weapons.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

Iran said a fourth tanker was hit by a mine in the Strait of Hormuz on 31 October, according to Anadolu Ajansı and Yeni Şafak English. No vessel name, flag, position or casualty details were given, and the claim was not independently confirmed.

Earlier in the week, a vessel was struck by an unknown projectile about 40 nautical miles from Iran's Lavan Island on 30 October and caught fire, according to UKMTO.

Earlier in the week, uKMTO reported, citing the vessel's Company Security Officer, that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile while completing an outbound transit of the Strait of Hormuz on 29 October at 1600 UTC.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

The Houthis said they attacked a Saudi tanker in the Red Sea on 31 October, according to EnergyNow.com and Geo News. No vessel name, position, weapon type, damage or casualty details were given, and the claim was not independently confirmed.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
