Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah III hit in Strait of Hormuz

Kazimah III struck by unknown projectile in Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, fire on board, crew safe. Ukraine claims Black Sea strike.

Key points

  • Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah III was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, UKMTO said.
  • A Panama-flagged tanker was reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 2 October with a fire on board, according to shipping media and security firm Ambrey, not UKMTO.
  • Ukraine's President Zelenskiy claimed Ukrainian forces struck a Russian-linked vessel in the Black Sea on 1 October.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

The Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah III was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, starting a fire, with the crew reported safe by UKMTO.

In the last 24 hours, one further tanker incident was reported in the Strait of Hormuz, and Ukraine claimed a strike on a vessel in the Black Sea. No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO Warning 147-26 reported that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, with a fire and the crew safe. Shipping media and security firm Vanguard Tech named the vessel as the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah III. Later reports also described a Panama-flagged tanker hit with fires on board in the same incident.

A Panama-flagged tanker was reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 2 October with a fire on board, according to shipping media and security firm Ambrey. No further details were given. Maritime security firm Ambrey reported that a Panama-flagged tanker was struck by a projectile about 5.3 nautical miles north-northwest of Kumzar on 2 October and reviewed imagery of smoke from the stern, but UKMTO has published no matching warning naming this vessel.

Black Sea

Ukrainian forces struck a Russian-linked vessel in the Black Sea on 1 October along with other targets, according to President Zelenskiy and RBC-Ukraine. No vessel name, type or damage details were given. Only Ukraine's president and Ukrainian outlets say a Russian vessel was struck, and no neutral or Russian confirmation of the strike was found.

What this means for crews

Vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz should follow UKMTO and JMIC guidance, maintain a strict lookout and report any suspicious approach to UKMTO. Ship managers should review company security procedures and ensure crews know the latest threat warnings. In the Black Sea, crews should remain vigilant and follow any instructions from local authorities.
