IMO confirms tanker JIF BARRACUDA damaged off Khor Fakkan on 18 October

The tanker was damaged with one seafarer injured northeast of Khor Fakkan, the IMO's confirmed incidents list says.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 22 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • The IMO lists the vessel as damaged with no pollution and one seafarer injured.
  • A report said the general cargo ship Able, attacked off Bulgaria, has sunk.
  • UKMTO reported a tanker struck by an unidentified projectile southeast of Iraq's Al Faw port on 21 October.
  • UKMTO reported a fire on a tanker about 11 nautical miles off Bukha, Oman, on 21 October, with all crew safe.

The IMO's confirmed incidents list records the tanker JIF BARRACUDA as damaged with one seafarer injured northeast of Khor Fakkan, UAE, on 18 October.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (12 the week before); 0 in the Black Sea (5 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The IMO lists the tanker as damaged with no pollution and one seafarer injured. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said the tanker was hit by a sea mine, a claim not confirmed by the IMO list. UKMTO warning 177-26 reports a tug struck by two projectiles northeast of Khor Fakkan on the same day.

UKMTO reported that a tanker was struck by an unidentified projectile in the northern Persian Gulf southeast of Iraq's Al Faw port on 21 October. TradeWinds identified a Greek product tanker as one of the latest targets in the Gulf attack, with no vessel name or damage given.

UKMTO reported that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile about 11 nautical miles off Bukha, Oman, near the Strait of Hormuz, on 21 October, causing a fire that was later extinguished with all crew safe. TradeWinds identified a Kuwaiti product tanker as one of the latest targets in the Gulf attack, with no vessel name or damage given.

A headline said Iran declared the Strait of Hormuz closed after an 'unauthorised' vessel was hit. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or attribution details were given.

Earlier in the week, the IMO's confirmed incidents list recorded the vessel LOYALTY as damaged with no pollution 78 nautical miles northeast of Al Jubail, Saudi Arabia, on 16 October.

Earlier in the week, UKMTO reported that a tanker was involved in an interaction with military forces about 121 nautical miles east of Duqm, Oman, on 19 October, with no damage, casualties or attribution given.

Earlier in the week, Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed a supertanker carrying about two million barrels of oil caught fire after hitting a naval mine south of the Strait of Hormuz on 20 October, and separately claimed two tankers were set ablaze by mines near Hormuz.

Earlier in the week, a Turkish headline reported that a huge oil tanker that had been attacked by a UAV passed through the Strait of Hormuz on 19 October, with no vessel name, flag, position, damage or attribution details given.

Black Sea

A report said the general cargo ship Able, one of the vessels attacked off Bulgaria's coast, has sunk. No date, position, cause or attribution details were given.

Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran drones struck dry cargo vessels in the Black Sea heading to Odesa and Chornomorsk and vessels in Reni port on 21 October, saying they carried military cargo for Ukraine. The claims come from the Russian side only, with no vessel names, flags, positions, damage or casualties given.

Earlier in the week, a Turkish headline reported that the tanker NELSA, which was attacked by a UAV in Russia, passed through the Bosphorus on 19 October, with no date, position, damage or attribution details given.

Earlier in the week, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck dry cargo ships carrying military and dual-use cargo in the Black Sea south and west of Odesa across 17 to 19 October, with no vessel names, flags, positions or independent confirmation given.

Earlier in the week, a headline reported escalating tensions in the Black Sea following drone strikes on commercial vessels on 19 October, with no vessel name, flag, position, damage or attribution details given.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

The Houthis announced a strike on a Saudi oil tanker on 21 October, according to a Yemen Online headline. No vessel name, position, damage or casualty information was given, and the claim is from the Houthi side only.

Earlier in the week, a headline reported that drone strikes stalled two fuel tankers bound for Kenya on 19 October, with no vessel names, flags, positions, damage details or attribution given, and the region not stated.
