Kuwaiti tanker Al Dasma struck near Strait of Hormuz

The Aframax tanker was hit by an unknown projectile 11 nautical miles west of Bukha, Oman, on 21 October, with the fire extinguished and all crew safe.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 22 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • UKMTO warning 179-26 reported the strike at 01:45 UTC and said the fire was extinguished.
  • The IMO list records one seafarer injured and no pollution on the Dutch-flagged tug.
  • Russia's defence ministry said the drones targeted vessels bound for Odesa and Chornomorsk.
  • Bulgaria's prime minister confirmed the Able and Alfa Watan were struck in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

The Kuwaiti-flagged Aframax tanker Al Dasma was struck by an unknown projectile about 11 nautical miles west of Bukha, Oman, on 21 October, UKMTO reported.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (12 the week before); 0 in the Black Sea (5 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The fire broke out after the strike at 01:45 UTC and was later extinguished, with all crew reported safe and the vessel now at anchor. UKMTO warning 179-26 gives the date as 21 October, while the IMO confirmed incidents list dates the strike 20 October. The projectile's origin is not known.

The Dutch-flagged tug JIF BARRACUDA was damaged northeast of Khor Fakkan, UAE, on 18 October, with one seafarer injured and no pollution, according to the IMO confirmed incidents list. UKMTO warning 177-26 reports the same incident as a tug struck by two projectiles.

Earlier in the week, uKMTO reported that a tanker was struck by an unidentified projectile in the northern Persian Gulf southeast of Iraq's Al Faw port on 21 October.

Earlier in the week, the IMO lists the Loyalty as hit near 78 NM northeast of Al Jubail, Saudi Arabia on 16 October.

Earlier in the week, uKMTO reported that a tanker was involved in an interaction with military forces about 121 nm east of Duqm, Oman, as part of ongoing military activity in the region.

Earlier in the week, iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed a supertanker carrying about two million barrels of oil caught fire after hitting a naval mine south of the Strait of Hormuz, and separately claimed two tankers were set ablaze by mines near Hormuz.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran drones struck dry cargo vessels in the Black Sea heading to Odesa and Chornomorsk, and vessels in Reni port, on 21 October, saying they carried military cargo for Ukraine. The claim gives no vessel names, flags, positions, damage or casualties, and comes from the Russian side only.

The Palau-flagged general cargo ship Able, hit 70 nautical miles east of Byala while carrying barley to Turkey on 6 October, drifted unmanned and sank off the Turkish coast after vanishing from radar late on 19 October, Bulgarian officials said.

Earlier in the week, russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck dry cargo ships carrying military and dual-use cargo in the Black Sea south and west of Odesa across 17 to 19 October.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.
