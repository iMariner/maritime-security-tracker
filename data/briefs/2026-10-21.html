Tanker JIF BARRACUDA damaged northeast of Khor Fakkan, IMO lists

The IMO lists the 18 October attack, which injured one seafarer and caused no pollution.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 06:06 UTC on 21 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • The IMO lists the tanker as damaged northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October, with one seafarer injured.
  • Novinite.com and The Sofia Globe reported that the general cargo ship Able had sunk off Bulgaria's coast.
  • A Turkish headline said the tanker NELSA, attacked by a drone in Russia, later passed through the Bosphorus.
  • Russia's defence ministry said it struck a dry cargo vessel heading to Odesa with military cargo on 21 October.

The IMO lists the tanker JIF BARRACUDA as damaged northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October, with one seafarer injured, according to its incidents list.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 2 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (12 the week before); 1 in the Black Sea (4 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The IMO lists the vessel as a tanker and reports no pollution from the attack. The IMO lists IRGC Navy (Iran) as saying the tanker was hit by a sea mine. UKMTO warning 177-26 reported a tug struck by two projectiles northeast of Khor Fakkan on the same day.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed on 20 October that a supertanker carrying about two million barrels of oil caught fire after striking a naval mine south of the Strait of Hormuz. The Guards separately claimed two tankers were set ablaze by mines near Hormuz. No vessel names, flags or independent confirmation were given.

A Turkish headline reported on 19 October that a large oil tanker attacked by a drone had transited the Strait of Hormuz. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or attribution was given.

The Guardian reported on 19 October that people were scouring the Strait of Hormuz for spilt cargo after Iran attacked container ships. No vessel names, flags, dates or positions were given.

Earlier in the week, india's shipping minister Sarbananda Sonowal named the crude oil tanker ST 3, with 15 Indian seafarers aboard, as struck by a projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 16 October; all were safe. An Indian shipping official also noted the Romania incident of 14 October.

Black Sea

Novinite.com and The Sofia Globe reported on 20 October that the general cargo ship Able, one of the vessels attacked off Bulgaria's coast, had sunk, according to a report. No date, position, cause or attribution was given.

A Turkish headline reported on 19 October that the tanker NELSA, attacked by a drone in Russia, had passed through the Bosphorus. No date, position, damage or attribution was given.

Russia's defence ministry claimed on 21 October that its forces struck a dry cargo vessel at sea en route to Odesa port carrying military cargo for Ukraine. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or casualty details were given, and the claim was not independently confirmed.

Russia's defence ministry claimed on 20 October that overnight drone strikes hit several dry cargo and container vessels in and around the Odesa region, including ships at sea heading to Odesa port and vessels unloading in Reni port. No vessel names, flags or damage details were given, and the claim was not independently confirmed.

Earlier in the week, on 19 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed a dry cargo ship carrying military and dual-use cargo was struck 130 km southwest of Odesa port.

Earlier in the week, on 19 October, a headline reported escalating tensions in the Black Sea following drone strikes on commercial vessels, without giving vessel details.

Earlier in the week, on 18 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a large dry cargo ship in the Black Sea, including a vessel carrying Ukrainian military cargo west of Odesa.

Earlier in the week, on 17 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a dry cargo ship carrying military and dual-use cargo about 40 km south of Odesa port.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Earlier in the week, on 19 October, a headline reported that drone strikes stalled two fuel tankers bound for Kenya, without giving vessel details.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
