UKMTO confirms tanker hit by projectile in Strait of Hormuz

A product tanker was struck on an outbound transit on 12 October, with three more vessels hit the previous day.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:40 UTC on 13 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • UKMTO's warning 168-26 said a product tanker was struck by an unknown projectile on 12 October.
  • Turkey's DHA reported the Fortis was towed to the anchorage off Giresun port on 12 October.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

UKMTO reported on 12 October that a product tanker was struck by an unknown projectile on an outbound transit in the Strait of Hormuz.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 5 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (9 the week before); 1 in the Black Sea (2 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO's warning 168-26 covers the product tanker struck on 12 October, and warning 169-26 covers a crude oil tanker struck outbound on 11 October. Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps-affiliated Tasnim published video of the Liberian-registered VLCC Al Maqam on fire, owned by ADNOC Logistics and Services per Seatrade Maritime; ADNOC has not commented and it is not confirmed the tanker was struck. UKMTO gave no vessel name, flag, position or damage details.

Earlier in the week, the IMO's confirmed incidents list recorded the Marshall Islands-flagged tanker Valthera hit at the Strait of Hormuz, Oman TTW, on 10 October, damaged with no pollution.

Black Sea

The Russian-flagged dry cargo ship Fortis, carrying 3,300 tonnes of chickpeas from Novorossiysk to Mersin, was found drifting off Keşap in Giresun province after its 10 crew abandoned it following a drone strike at sea, Turkey's DHA reported via Milliyet and Hürriyet. The unmanned ship was towed to the anchorage off Giresun port on 12 October. The reports give no attack date, position, attacker or casualty.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
