Iran claims strike on ADNOC tanker in Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO issued four alerts for the Strait of Hormuz on 12 October; no vessel name, flag or damage details were given.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:40 UTC on 13 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Iran's claim of striking an ADNOC tanker came as UKMTO issued four alerts for the strait.
  • A Russian-flagged ship drifted off Giresun after a drone hit on 12 October, Turkish outlets reported.
  • Satellite imagery showed a 20-kilometre oil slick behind a tanker off Sochi.
  • Vietnam's maritime authority said no Vietnamese-flagged ship was attacked in the strait.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

Iran claimed it struck an ADNOC tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on 12 October, according to Haber.com, as UKMTO issued four attack alerts for the strait.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (9 the week before); 2 in the Black Sea (2 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

At least 18 attacks on ships have been reported in the strait since early October, according to Seatrade Maritime. No vessel name, flag, position or damage details were given. It was not confirmed which vessel was hit.

Vietnam's maritime authority rejected reports that a Vietnamese-flagged ship was attacked in the Strait of Hormuz on 12 October, according to Tuoi Tre News. The authority denies the reported attack occurred.

Earlier in the week, the IMO listed VALTHERA as hit at Strait of Hormuz, Oman TTW on 10 October, damaged with no pollution.

Black Sea

A Russian-flagged ship was hit by a drone attack off Giresun, Turkey, on 12 October and drifted before being towed to Giresun, according to Muhalif and Yeni Şafak. No vessel name or further details were given.

Satellite imagery showed a 20-kilometre oil slick trailing a tanker after it was attacked off Sochi on 12 October, according to Mid-Day. No vessel name, flag, position, attacker or casualty details were given.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
