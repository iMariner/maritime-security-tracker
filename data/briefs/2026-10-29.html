Container ship Contship Jet struck by sea drones off Bulgaria

The Liberian-flagged vessel was hit by three drones en route to Constanța, Romania, and her crew was evacuated, the owner and government said.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 06:05 UTC on 29 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • The Contship Jet was bound for Constanța when three drones struck her, the owner and government said.
  • Yemen's Houthis said they targeted two Saudi tankers in the Red Sea on 28 October.
  • A French Navy frigate intercepted a ballistic missile near a Belgian-flagged ship in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, Baird Maritime reported.
  • Capt. Todor Georgiev said the drones that hit a Greek-flagged vessel off Bulgaria on 28 October were Russian.

Sea drones struck the container ship Contship Jet off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast on 28 October, according to the ship's owner and the government, and the crew was reported safe and ashore.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 3 in the Black Sea (0 the week before); 2 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (6 the week before); 2 in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden (0 the week before).

Black Sea

The Liberian-flagged Contship Jet was bound for Constanța, Romania, when three drones struck her, according to the ship's owner and the government. Romania's Minister of National Defence, Ștefan-Radu Oprea, commented on the attack. Russia's defence ministry said it attacked a transport carrying cargo for Ukraine's armed forces. The extent of the damage was not immediately clear.

A Romanian outlet reported that a Greek-flagged vessel was hit by drones off the Bulgarian coast on 28 October. No vessel name, position, damage or casualty details were given. Capt. Todor Georgiev said the drones were Russian.

Russia's defence ministry said its drones struck a general cargo vessel at sea on 29 October as it carried military and dual-use cargo to Odesa port. The ministry gave no vessel name, flag, position, damage details or casualties.

EU Today reported that drone attacks are targeting commercial shipping in the Black Sea as NATO confronts a growing threat there. No specific vessel, date, position, damage or casualty details were given.

A Turkish outlet reported that the Bosphorus was closed to traffic because of a tanker attacked by a drone in the Black Sea. No vessel name, flag, date or position of the attack was given.

Earlier in the week, on 26 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck dry cargo vessels bound for Odesa and Chornomorsk carrying military equipment and hit port infrastructure at Chornomorsk, naming no ship.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

Earlier in the week, on 26 October, UKMTO recorded a tanker disabled by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz with all crew safe.

Earlier in the week, on 26 October, UKMTO warned that a vessel was on fire off Iraq's Al-Faw coast, with the name, cause and crew status unknown.

Earlier in the week, on 27 October, Iran's army said an unnamed oil tanker struck naval mines and caught fire east of the Strait of Hormuz, carried by IRNA with no independent confirmation.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Yemen's Houthis said they targeted two Saudi tankers in the Red Sea on 28 October, and a source said the tanker Encelia was hit by a missile. No position, damage details or casualties were given.

A French Navy frigate intercepted a ballistic missile near a Belgian-flagged ship in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, according to a Baird Maritime headline. No vessel name, date, damage or casualty details were given.
