Liberian cargo ship hit in Odesa, one crew killed

Russian strike on a Liberian-flagged cargo vessel in Odesa region killed one crew member and injured three, Ukraine's Sea Ports Administration said.

Key points

  • A Liberian-flagged cargo ship was struck in a port in the Odesa region on 3 October, Ukraine's Sea Ports Administration said.
  • One motorman was killed, three crew were injured and 13 were evacuated, the administration said.
  • A Turkish ship was attacked in the Black Sea on 3 October, with an Albanian engineer reported missing, according to reports.
  • Iran targeted ships in the Strait of Hormuz with 360 missile and drone strikes, according to a report.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

A Liberian-flagged general cargo vessel was struck in a port in the Odesa region on 3 October, killing one crew member and injuring three, according to Ukraine's Sea Ports Administration.

The attack was one of two incidents in the Black Sea reported in the last 24 hours, while a report described a large cumulative tally of Iranian strikes near the Strait of Hormuz. No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

Strait of Hormuz

Iran targeted ships in the Strait of Hormuz with 360 missile and drone strikes, according to a report cited by bankingnews. No vessel names, flags or damage details were given. The figure is a cumulative tally from an unnamed Western official, not a single strike.

Black Sea

The Liberian-flagged general cargo vessel was hit in a port in the Odesa region on 3 October, Ukraine's Sea Ports Administration said. One motorman was killed, three crew were injured and 13 were evacuated. ZN.ua reported the strike was carried out by a kamikaze drone.

A Turkish ship was attacked in the Black Sea on 3 October, according to Gazeta Sot and kosovarja-ks.com. An Albanian engineer aboard is reported missing. No vessel name, attacker or further details were given.

What this means for crews

Crews in the Black Sea and Strait of Hormuz should follow UKMTO and JMIC guidance and their company security procedures. Report any suspicious activity to UKMTO without delay. Ship managers should review risk assessments for these areas before transit.
