Liberian-flagged cargo ship struck in Odesa region, one killed

A Liberian-flagged cargo vessel was struck in Odesa region on 3 October, killing one crew member and injuring three, Ukraine's Sea Ports Administration said.

Key points

  • A Liberian-flagged cargo ship was struck in Odesa region on 3 October, Ukraine's Sea Ports Administration said.
  • One motorman was killed, three crew were injured and 13 were evacuated, the administration said.
  • A Turkish cargo ship was attacked in the Black Sea on 3 October, with one Albanian engineer reported missing.
  • A report said Iran carried out about 360 missile and drone strikes on shipping near the Strait of Hormuz since early August.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

A Liberian-flagged general cargo vessel was struck in a port in the Odesa region on 3 October, killing one crew member and injuring three, according to the Ukrainian Sea Ports Administration.

The attack was the most serious of a small number of incidents reported in the last 24 hours. Separately, a Turkish cargo ship was attacked in the Black Sea, and a report described a large cumulative tally of Iranian strikes near the Strait of Hormuz. No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

Iran has targeted ships in the Strait of Hormuz with about 360 missile and drone strikes, according to a report cited by Bankingnews. The report gave no vessel names, flags or damage details. The New York Times, cited by Bankingnews, described the figure as a cumulative tally from an unnamed Western official rather than a single strike.

UKMTO and JMIC have confirmed repeated Iranian attacks on vessels in the area, the report said.

Black Sea

A Liberian-flagged general cargo vessel was struck in a port in the Odesa region on 3 October, the Ukrainian Sea Ports Administration said. One motorman was killed, three crew were injured and 13 were evacuated, it said. ZN.ua reported the strike was carried out by a kamikaze drone.

A Turkish general cargo ship was attacked in the Black Sea on 3 October, according to Gazeta Sot and kosovarja-ks.com. An Albanian engineer aboard the ship is reported missing. No vessel name, attacker or further details were given.

What this means for crews

Crews in the Black Sea and the Strait of Hormuz should follow UKMTO and JMIC guidance and their company security procedures. Report any suspicious approach or attack to UKMTO without delay. Ship managers ashore should keep watch schedules and crew musters up to date.
