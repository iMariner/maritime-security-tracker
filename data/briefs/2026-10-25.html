Oil-bulk-ore carrier Khoryv sinks off Romania, one dead and three missing

The Comoros-flagged vessel was hit by a drone about 13 nautical miles off Constanța on 23 October, Romanian authorities said.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 25 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Romania's interior ministry said the drone strike caused major damage and a fire on the Khoryv.
  • WANA News Agency reported the Hormuz explosion but gave no vessel name, flag or position.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

The oil-bulk-ore carrier Khoryv sank after a drone strike about 13 nautical miles off Constanța, Romania, on 23 October, killing one crew member and leaving three missing, Romanian authorities said.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 1 in the Black Sea (5 the week before); 4 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (6 the week before).

Black Sea

Nine crew were rescued. Romania's interior ministry said the strike caused major damage and a fire, and the Romanian Naval Authority is leading the search for the three missing. Romanian officials blamed Russia. The vessel's flag was Comoros.

Earlier in the week, russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran drones struck dry cargo ships and a container ship in the Black Sea between 21 and 23 October, saying they carried military cargo for Ukraine. Separate statements said drones struck port infrastructure and a ship at Vilkovo port, with no vessel names or damage details given.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

WANA News Agency reported an explosion and possible damage to a giant oil tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on 24 October. No vessel name, flag, position or attribution was given.

Earlier in the week, uKMTO reported that the tanker Al Dasma was struck by an unknown projectile about 11 nautical miles off Bukha, Oman, starting a fire that was extinguished with all crew safe. UKMTO warning 179-26 gives the date as 21 October, while the IMO confirmed incidents list records the strike on 20 October.

Earlier in the week, uKMTO reported that the tanker Soyia was struck by an unidentified projectile 30 nautical miles southeast of Iraq's Al Faw port on 20 October, with all crew safe and no damage or environmental impact reported.

Earlier in the week, the IMO confirmed incidents list records the Hong Kong-flagged tanker Chloe (Minerva V), IMO 9315367, as damaged with no pollution 121 nautical miles off the coast of Oman on 19 October.

Earlier in the week, uKMTO received a time-late report that a tanker was hit by an unknown projectile 7 nautical miles northeast of Limah, Oman, on 22 October while transiting outbound, and was able to continue its voyage.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.
