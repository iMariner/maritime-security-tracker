Tanker catches fire in Strait of Hormuz after explosives attached to hull

The vessel was hit by explosives attached to a small boat in the Strait of Hormuz on 30 October, with crew reported safe, according to UKMTO.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 30 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • The outlet reported the tanker's crew were safe after the fire in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Iran's IRGC claimed three oil tankers struck mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz on 29 October.
  • Russia's defence ministry said its drones struck a dry cargo vessel bound for Odesa on 29 October.
  • A headline reported a Houthi attack near Ras Tanura Terminal on 29 October that pushed oil prices up.

A tanker caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz on 30 October after explosives were attached to its hull, according to the outlet, with the crew reported safe.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 4 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (4 the week before); 2 in the Black Sea (1 the week before); 1 in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden (0 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The vessel's name, flag and position were not given. The outlet described the attack as a new method used by Iran against commercial shipping. UKMTO did not name the vessel.

Iran's IRGC claimed that three oil tankers struck mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz on 29 October, and UKMTO reported a vessel on fire there. A separate headline reported a tanker struck by a projectile in Hormuz as the attack tally grew, with no vessel named.

Earlier in the week, on 26 October, a projectile strike disabled a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz, and Lloyd's List reported the bulk carrier Cape Dao sank after the attack; the IMO confirmed register names the Leonora Kosan, IMO 9390953, damaged 6 nautical miles northeast of Limah on 23 October, while the Cape Dao named in the issue sank on 23 September.

Earlier in the week, on 26 October, UKMTO issued a late warning that a vessel was on fire off Iraq's Al-Faw coast, with no vessel name, flag, cause of the fire, damage details or casualties given.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a dry cargo vessel at sea carrying military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa port on 29 October. It also claimed a vessel bound for Chornomorsk and strikes on port infrastructure. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or casualties were given, and the claims were not independently confirmed.

A Turkish maritime news outlet reported that Türkiye warned Russia and Ukraine against attacks on ships on 29 October. No specific vessel, position, damage or casualty details were given.

A Turkish outlet reported an attack on a ship during a NATO meeting on 29 October, saying a French aircraft warned but the attack could not be prevented. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or casualty details were given.

Earlier in the week, on 28 October, a Greek container ship sank after being hit by naval drones off Bulgaria, according to the Bulgarian government, with all crew rescued.

Earlier in the week, on 28 October, an Arabic-language headline reported that an Israeli cargo ship sank after being targeted by drones in the Black Sea, with no vessel name, position or casualty details given.

Earlier in the week, on 28 October, sea drones struck a container ship off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast, according to the ship's owner and the government, with the crew reported safe and ashore.

Earlier in the week, on 28 October, a headline reported that drone attacks are targeting commercial shipping in the Black Sea as NATO confronts a growing threat there, with no specific vessel or details given.

Earlier in the week, on 26 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck dry cargo vessels bound for Odesa and Chornomorsk that it said carried military equipment, and hit port infrastructure at Chornomorsk, with no vessel named and no independent confirmation.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

A headline reported a Houthi attack near Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura Terminal on 29 October that pushed oil prices up. No vessel name, flag, type, damage or casualty details were given.

Earlier in the week, on 28 October, Yemen's Houthis claimed they targeted two Saudi tankers in the Red Sea, and a source said the tanker Encelia was hit by a missile.

Earlier in the week, on 28 October, a headline reported an attack targeting a Liberia-flagged cargo ship off the coast of Yemen, with no vessel name, position, damage, casualties or attribution given.
