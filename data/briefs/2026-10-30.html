Tanker Louise 1 hit by explosives in Strait of Hormuz, crew safe

The Panama flagged tanker suffered an engine room fire after two devices were attached by a small boat northeast of Khasab, UKMTO said.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 30 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • UKMTO said two explosive devices were attached by a small boat, starting an engine room fire that was put out.
  • Russia's defence ministry said its drones struck a dry cargo vessel bound for Odesa carrying military cargo.
  • Iran's IRGC claimed three oil tankers struck mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz, a claim the AP could not confirm.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

The Panama flagged tanker Louise 1 was hit by explosives attached to a small boat 25 nautical miles northeast of Khasab in the Strait of Hormuz on 29 October, UKMTO said, with the crew safe.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 2 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (5 the week before); 1 in the Black Sea (1 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO reported that two explosive devices were attached by a small boat at 22:12 UTC on 29 October, starting an engine room fire that was put out. The ship is continuing her voyage, UKMTO said. Seatrade Maritime, citing Vanguard Tech, named the vessel as the Panama flagged tanker Louise 1.

Iran's IRGC claimed on 29 October that three oil tankers struck mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz, a claim the AP could not confirm. UKMTO separately reported a dhow on fire 9 nautical miles north of Khasab the same day, with its crew evacuated, a vessel regional authorities identified and not one of the three tankers in the IRGC claim.

Earlier in the week, uKMTO warned on 26 October that a vessel was on fire 63 nautical miles southeast of Al-Faw off Iraq's coast, with the name, cause, damage and crew status unknown.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a dry cargo vessel at sea carrying military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa port on 29 October. It gave no vessel name, flag, position, damage or casualties, and the claim was not independently confirmed.

Earlier in the week, the Liberia flagged container ship Contship Jet, carrying 421 containers from Haifa to Constanta, sank after three sea drones struck her about 17 nautical miles east of Cape Kaliakra on 28 October, Contships Management, the Bulgarian government and Reuters reported. All 18 crew, 14 Filipinos and 4 Ukrainians, were rescued with no deaths.

Earlier in the week, russia's defence ministry claimed on 26 October that its drones struck dry cargo vessels bound for Odesa and Chornomorsk carrying military equipment, and hit port infrastructure at Chornomorsk, naming no ship and with Reuters unable to verify the claim.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
