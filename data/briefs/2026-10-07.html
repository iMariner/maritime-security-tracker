Tanker On Peace hit in Hormuz as drone sinks Alfa Watan off Bulgaria

Twelve crew injured on the Panama-flagged tanker in the Strait of Hormuz, while a drone attack off Bulgaria sank the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 06:01 UTC on 7 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Panama-flagged tanker On Peace hit in Strait of Hormuz on 6 October, 12 crew injured, according to the British maritime authority UKMTO.
  • Togo-flagged general cargo ship Alfa Watan sank off Bulgaria on 6 October after drone attack; crew missing, Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed.
  • Palau-flagged Able also hit off Bulgaria and caught fire, crew rescued, the Bulgarian minister said; no state confirmed responsible.
  • Six merchant ships reported hit in Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in seven days, according to the iMariners tracker.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

The Panama-flagged tanker On Peace was hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 6 October, injuring 12 crew, according to the British maritime authority UKMTO.

The attack is the sixth merchant ship reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in seven days, the iMariners tracker shows. In the Black Sea, drones struck two ships off Bulgaria on the same day, one of which sank with its crew missing.

Strait of Hormuz

The tanker On Peace suffered an engine fire after being struck on 6 October, UKMTO said. At least 12 seafarers were injured, and the Indian government condemned the incident, saying 11 of its nationals were among the injured. WANA News Agency reported an oil tanker hit by an unidentified projectile while exiting the Strait of Hormuz.

Separately, the Greek-flagged LNG carrier Maran Gas Mystras was hit around the waterline by an unidentified projectile while leaving the Strait of Hormuz on 4 October, according to Hellenic Shipping News and VesselFinder. The vessel is operated by Maran Gas Maritime. No group has claimed responsibility.

Black Sea

The Togo-flagged general cargo ship Alfa Watan sank off Bulgaria on 6 October after a drone attack, Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed. Its crew is missing. The Palau-flagged general cargo ship Able was also hit and caught fire, with its crew rescued, the Bulgarian minister said. No state has been confirmed responsible.

In a separate attack on 6 October, a Russian drone struck a Marshall Islands-flagged commercial vessel loaded with rapeseed oil in the Black Sea in the Odesa region, setting the superstructure on fire and killing one person and injuring seven, the Odesa Regional Military Administration said. India condemned the attack after three of its nationals were injured. Other reports gave differing casualty figures.

Also on 6 October, the Liberian-flagged oil tanker Aframax Rio was attacked by uncrewed boats off the coast of Sochi, Russia's Ministry of Transport confirmed. Its crew was rescued, with no injuries reported and 23 Indian crew members rescued. Reports said the tanker caught fire and oil burned in the Black Sea, with beaches near Sirius temporarily closed.

Ukraine's President claimed that a Russian drone attack sank a Turkish ship in the Black Sea on 5 October. No vessel name, exact position, damage details or casualty figures were given.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO. Follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
