Bulgaria says drones sank Alfa Watan as tanker On Peace hit in Hormuz

Togo-flagged general cargo ship sank off Bulgaria with crew missing, while 12 seafarers were injured on a Panama-flagged tanker in the Strait of Hormuz.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:41 UTC on 7 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Togo-flagged Alfa Watan sank off Bulgaria on 6 October, crew missing, Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed.
  • Panama-flagged tanker On Peace suffered an engine fire in the Strait of Hormuz on 6 October, injuring 12 crew.
  • Marshall Islands-flagged cargo ship hit by drone in Odesa region on 6 October, one killed and seven injured.
  • Liberian-flagged tanker Aframax Rio attacked by sea drones off Sochi on 6 October, 23 Indian crew rescued.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

Drones struck two Turkish-owned general cargo ships off Bulgaria on 6 October, sinking the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan and leaving its crew missing, according to Türkiye Today.

The attacks add to a sharp rise in merchant shipping incidents in the Black Sea and the Strait of Hormuz. The iMariners tracker has recorded six merchant ships reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in the last seven days, and five in the Black Sea over the same period.

Black Sea

Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed that drones struck two merchant vessels in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone on 6 October, the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan sinking and the Palau-flagged Able catching fire. No state has been confirmed as responsible. The Able's crew was rescued, according to a Bulgarian minister.

In the Odesa region, a Marshall Islands-flagged general cargo ship loaded with rapeseed oil was struck by a Russian drone on 6 October, the Odesa Regional Military Administration said. The superstructure caught fire, killing one person and injuring seven, according to the administration. India condemned the attack after three of its nationals were injured, while other reports gave differing casualty figures.

Russia's Ministry of Transport confirmed that the Liberian-flagged oil tanker Aframax Rio was attacked by uncrewed boats off Sochi on 6 October and its crew was rescued. The Sochi mayor said oil was burning on the water after the attack, and beaches near Sirius were temporarily closed. Reports said 23 Indian crew were rescued and no injuries were reported.

A Greek tanker was ablaze off Russia on 6 October in what TradeWinds News described as a presumed Ukrainian sea drone strike. No vessel name, position or casualty details were given.

Strait of Hormuz

At least 12 seafarers were injured in an attack on the Panama-flagged tanker On Peace in the Strait of Hormuz on 6 October, the British maritime authority UKMTO said. The Indian government condemned the incident, saying 11 of its nationals were among the injured. WANA News Agency reported an oil tanker hit by an unidentified projectile while exiting the Strait of Hormuz.

The Greek-flagged LNG carrier Maran Gas Mystras, operated by Maran Gas Maritime, was struck by an unidentified projectile while leaving the Strait of Hormuz on 4 October, according to Hellenic Shipping News. The vessel was hit around the waterline. The cause of the explosion has not been established.

The IMO lists the Ghana Prosperity (IMO 9732541) as hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 3 October, with damage and no pollution reported.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
