Tanker On Peace hit in Hormuz on 5 October; 12 injured, 11 Indian, India says

Panama-flagged tanker struck on 5 October, with an engine room fire and 12 injured reported a day later, India's government says.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 07:00 UTC on 7 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Panama-flagged tanker On Peace was hit by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 5 October, UKMTO says.
  • The strike caused an engine room fire; 12 crew were injured, 11 of them Indian, India's government says.
  • Greek LNG carrier Maran Gas Mystras was hit around the waterline in the Strait of Hormuz on 4 October, Hellenic Shipping News and vesselfinder.com report.
  • Drones hit two Turkish-owned ships off Bulgaria on 6 October; the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan sank, Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

The Panama-flagged tanker On Peace was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 5 October, causing an engine room fire, UKMTO said. India's government said 12 crew were injured, 11 of them Indian, in a statement reported on 6 October.

It is the sixth merchant ship reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in seven days, according to the iMariners tracker. The tracker also lists five merchant ships reported hit in the Black Sea in the same period.

Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO reported the strike on On Peace on 5 October and said there were no casualties at first. India's government said on 6 October that 12 crew were injured, 11 of them Indian. The attacker and cause are not yet known.

The Greek-flagged LNG carrier Maran Gas Mystras, operated by Maran Gas Maritime, was hit around the waterline by an unidentified projectile while leaving the Strait of Hormuz on 4 October, according to Hellenic Shipping News and vesselfinder.com. The cause is not yet known.

The IMO lists Ghana Prosperity (IMO 9732541) as hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 3 October, with damage and no pollution. No further details were given.

Black Sea

Drones hit two Turkish-owned ships off Bulgaria on 6 October, Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed. The Togo-flagged general cargo ship Alfa Watan sank and its crew is missing, while the Palau-flagged general cargo ship Able caught fire and its crew was rescued. No state has been confirmed as responsible.

A Russian drone struck a Marshall Islands-flagged commercial vessel loaded with rapeseed oil in the Black Sea on 6 October, the Odesa Regional Military Administration said. The superstructure caught fire, one person was killed and seven were injured. India condemned the attack after three of its nationals were injured, while other reports gave differing casualty figures.

The Liberian-flagged tanker Aframax Rio was attacked by uncrewed boats off the coast of Sochi on 6 October, Russia's Ministry of Transport confirmed. Oil burned on the water and beaches near Sirius were temporarily closed, reports said. All 23 crew, all Indian, were rescued with no injuries reported.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
