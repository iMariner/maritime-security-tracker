Crew of Royad Mammadov rescued after Black Sea drone strike

The Saint Kitts and Nevis flagged general cargo ship caught fire near Romania's Pescăruș platform, with 11 rescued and two dead.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 6 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • General cargo ship Royad Mammadov caught fire after a drone strike near Romania on 5 October.
  • Eleven seafarers were rescued and two were found dead, Romanian maritime rescue ARSVOM said.
  • The ship is Saint Kitts and Nevis flagged and Turkish owned, according to iMariners checks.
  • Ukrainian President Zelensky blamed Russia for the attack on the Turkish ship.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf or the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

The Saint Kitts and Nevis flagged general cargo ship Royad Mammadov caught fire after a drone strike near the Pescăruș platform off Romania on 5 October, prompting a rescue that recovered 11 seafarers and two deceased, Romanian maritime rescue ARSVOM said.

The incident is one of two merchant ships reported hit in the Black Sea in seven days, according to the iMariners tracker. No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf or the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

Black Sea

The Royad Mammadov, a Turkish-owned general cargo vessel carrying grain, was about 20 nautical miles off Romania when it caught fire and later sank, according to iMariners checks. ARSVOM said 13 sailors were on board; 11 were rescued and two were found dead. Ukrainian President Zelensky blamed Russia for the attack on the Turkish ship, while Romanian officials condemned attacks on civilian vessels.

Romanian authorities have not confirmed the drone cause or any attribution, and the attacker has not been independently verified. Kyiv Post reported that Russian drones sank a Turkish-owned grain ship, without naming the vessel or giving an exact position. Ukraine said Russia killed civilians in an attack on a ship in the Black Sea, without giving further details. Türkinform described an attack on a Turkish-linked ship as a disaster, and NationalTurk reported that a Turkish ship was attacked off Romania, killing two sailors.
