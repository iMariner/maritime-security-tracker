US jet disables Panama-flagged bulk carrier Ocean Molica in Gulf of Oman

CENTCOM said a fighter jet struck the Panama-flagged bulk carrier Ocean Molica, disabling its propulsion without harming the crew.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 11 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • CENTCOM said its fighter jet hit the Ocean Molica's stern with a precision munition.
  • Russia's defence ministry said it struck a general cargo ship bound for Odesa with military goods.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

CENTCOM said a US fighter jet struck the Panama-flagged bulk carrier Ocean Molica in the Gulf of Oman on 10 October, disabling its propulsion without harming the crew.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 5 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (11 the week before); 2 in the Black Sea (0 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

CENTCOM said the vessel, also known as the Arika Sun, had left an Iranian port and was struck on its stern with a precision munition. Reuters, Dawn and VesselFinder give the ship as a Panama-flagged, 1996-built bulk carrier of 70,296 dwt. UKMTO advisory 163-26 reports the same tanker and military forces incident in the Gulf of Oman the same day and names no vessel.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed on 10 October that its forces struck a general cargo ship in the north-western Black Sea as it carried military goods toward Odesa, and claimed a second vessel was hit in Ilyichevsk port. RIA Novosti, TASS and CGTN carried the claim. No ship was named and there is no independent confirmation.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.

Corrections

Our 8 October brief reported a Houthi claim about a Saudi oil tanker off Yanbu; the check found the claim belongs to the 5 August 2026 attack on the Wafa, so no such attack happened off Yanbu on 6 October.
