Cargo ship struck by drone drifts off Giresun, engineer missing

The general cargo vessel was hit in the Black Sea and drifted to Turkish waters, with a Turkish engineer reported missing.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 11 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Russia's defence ministry claimed it struck a general cargo ship carrying military goods toward Odesa.
  • UKMTO said it received a report of a tanker incident with military forces in the Gulf of Oman.
  • EnergyNow reported a cargo ship suspected an attack while crossing the Strait of Hormuz.
  • The Houthis said they attacked a Saudi tanker in the Red Sea, EnergyNow.com reported.

A general cargo ship was struck by a drone in the Black Sea on 10 October and drifted to waters off Giresun, Turkey, with a Turkish engineer reported missing, according to CGTN and Dva Mayora.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 2 in the Black Sea (0 the week before); 5 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (11 the week before); 1 in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden (0 the week before).

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry separately claimed it struck a general cargo ship delivering military goods toward Odesa, according to RIA Novosti. Saray Medya reported that a ship whose captain was killed in a drone attack arrived at Trabzon, without giving a vessel name, flag or position. The vessel's name, flag and owner have not been given.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO received a report of an incident involving a tanker and military forces in the Gulf of Oman on 10 October. Authorities are aware and investigations are ongoing, UKMTO said. The tanker's name, flag and the nature of the incident were not given.

A cargo ship reported a suspected attack while crossing the Strait of Hormuz on 10 October, according to EnergyNow. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or attacker details were given.

The US said a fighter jet struck a cargo ship in the Gulf of Oman on 10 October that was trying to break an Iran blockade, according to the Financial Times and TASS. No vessel name, flag or damage details were given.

Earlier in the week, on 9 October, a headline report said the US struck Iran after a drone attack on a cargo ship in the Strait of Hormuz, according to KATU.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

The Houthis said they attacked a Saudi tanker in the Red Sea on 10 October, according to EnergyNow.com. No vessel name, position or damage details were given.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.

Corrections

Our 8 October brief reported the Houthis announced they targeted a Saudi oil vessel off the coast of Yanbu; the only source is Al Mahriah's report of the Houthi claim on 5 August 2026 about the Saudi tanker Wafa off Yanbu, and no attack on a Saudi oil vessel off Yanbu was reported on 6 October.
