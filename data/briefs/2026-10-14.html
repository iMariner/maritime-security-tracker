Bulgarian media name sunken ship Azov Concord after Black Sea drone attack

Bulgarian media identified the sunken general cargo ship as the Azov Concord, with 11 crew missing after drones struck off Bulgaria's coast.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:40 UTC on 14 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Bulgarian media named the sunken general cargo ship as the Azov Concord, with 11 crew missing.
  • Bulgaria's Prime Minister said the Chelsea 3 was hit by three drones east of Byala.
  • A maritime agency reported a container ship and a tanker struck in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Russia's defence ministry claimed it struck a dry cargo ship in the Bug Estuary.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

Bulgarian media identified the sunken general cargo ship as the Azov Concord after drones struck off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast on 13 October, Bulgarian media said.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 3 in the Black Sea (2 the week before); 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (10 the week before).

Black Sea

Bulgarian media identified the sunken general cargo ship as the Azov Concord, and a maritime source said the vessel was in the search and rescue rather than hit. The President's office named the hull HS Dignity, Guinea-Bissau flagged, so its identity and flag are not settled. Bulgaria's Interior Ministry said the sunken ship's crew were mainly Syrians with three Egyptians.

Bulgaria's Prime Minister and Vanguard Tech, per Seatrade, reported the Maltese-flagged, Ukrainian-owned general cargo ship Chelsea 3 was hit by three drones 18 nautical miles east of Byala on 13 October, leaving her drifting without her crew. All 15 crew were rescued and five were injured. Azerbaijan confirmed eight of its citizens were rescued from the Chelsea 3.

Russia's defence ministry claimed its forces struck a dry cargo ship in the Bug Estuary on 13 October that was delivering military and dual-use cargo to Mykolaiv port. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or casualty details were given.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

A maritime agency reported that a container ship and a tanker were struck by unknown projectiles in the Strait of Hormuz on 13 October. Iran's Revolutionary Guards separately claimed to have struck a vessel in the Strait of Hormuz the same day, with no vessel names, flags, positions or damage details given.

UKMTO reported a tanker hit by multiple projectiles about 51 nautical miles off Qatar in the Arabian Gulf on 7 October, with casualties reported.

Earlier in the week, the IMO lists the Angola Prosperity as hit near 8NM from the coast of Oman on 11 October.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.
