Cargo ship Azov Concord sunk off Bulgaria, 11 crew missing

The Ukrainian-flagged general cargo ship was struck by drones off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast, Bulgaria's Prime Minister said.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:40 UTC on 14 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Bulgaria's Prime Minister said 11 crew from the Azov Concord are missing.
  • A Russian Telegram channel named the Maltese-flagged Chelsea-7 as attacked east of Varna.
  • Balkanweb.com said an Albanian engineer is missing from a Turkish ship in a Russian port.
  • A maritime agency reported a container ship and a tanker struck in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

Bulgaria's Prime Minister said drones struck the Ukrainian-flagged general cargo ship Azov Concord off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast on 13 October, sinking it and leaving 11 crew missing.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 6 in the Black Sea (2 the week before); 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (10 the week before).

Black Sea

Bulgaria's Prime Minister said the Azov Concord sank after drones struck it off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast on 13 October, leaving 11 crew missing. Azerbaijan said eight of its citizens were rescued. Bulgaria summoned Russia's ambassador over the attack, which many outlets reported without naming the ships.

A Russian Telegram channel said the Maltese-flagged general cargo ship Chelsea-7, owned by an Odesa-based Ukrainian company, was attacked east of Varna on 13 October. It said the vessel regularly calls at Ukrainian Danube ports and that the circumstances are not yet established.

An Albanian engineer is missing after a drone attack on a Turkish ship in a Russian port on 13 October, according to Balkanweb.com. The family is appealing to the government for information.

A Ukrainian news outlet reported that a vessel from Odesa was attacked off the coast of Bulgaria on 13 October. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or attacker was given.

The Krasnodar Krai Operational HQ and Morspassluzhba said no oil product spill was reported after an attack on a tanker in the Black Sea off the coast of Kuban on 13 October. No vessel name, flag, position or attacker was given.

Russia's defence ministry claimed its forces struck a dry cargo ship in the Bug Estuary on 13 October as it was delivering military and dual-use cargo to Mykolaiv port. No vessel name, flag, position, damage or casualty details were given.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

A maritime agency reported that a container ship and a tanker were struck by unknown projectiles in the Strait of Hormuz on 13 October. Iran's Revolutionary Guards separately claimed to have struck a vessel in the Strait of Hormuz the same day, with no vessel names, flags, positions or damage details given.

Inquirer.net reported that a Filipino seafarer injured in a tanker attack in the Persian Gulf received aid from the DMW. No vessel name, flag, position, date or attacker were given.

Firstpost reported that Iran unleashed anti-ship missiles in what it described as Iran's biggest attack in the Strait of Hormuz, hitting six vessels. No vessel names, flags, positions or damage details were given.

Earlier in the week, the IMO listed Angola Prosperity as hit 8NM from the coast of Oman on 11 October, damaged with no pollution.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.
