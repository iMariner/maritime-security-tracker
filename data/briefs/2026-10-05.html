Tanker Lipsi hit in Strait of Hormuz as blasts reported off Yemen

UKMTO says the Liberia-flagged LR2 tanker's engine room was damaged on 4 October; a second tanker reported explosions south of Al-Mokha.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 06:18 UTC on 5 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Liberia-flagged LR2 tanker Lipsi struck by unknown projectile in Strait of Hormuz on 4 October.
  • Engine room damaged; crew reported safe by UKMTO.
  • Tanker reports multiple explosions about 60 nm south of Al-Mokha, Yemen, on 4 October.
  • Fifth merchant ship reported hit in Hormuz in seven days, per iMariners tracker.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships reported in the Black Sea in the last 24 hours.

The Liberia-flagged LR2 tanker Lipsi was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 4 October, damaging its engine room, with the crew reported safe by UKMTO.

It is the fifth merchant ship reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in seven days, according to the iMariners tracker. In the Red Sea, a tanker reported multiple explosions about 60 nautical miles south of Al-Mokha, Yemen, on the same day.

Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO said the tanker was hit by an unknown projectile, causing damage to the engine room. The crew were reported safe. Maritime security firm Vanguard Tech identified the vessel as Lipsi, a new LR2 tanker linked to a Greek operator and a Chinese owner, according to the firm. The cause of the explosion has not been confirmed.

Red Sea

A tanker reported multiple explosions and an attempted attack near the vessel about 60 nautical miles south of Al-Mokha, near Bab el-Mandeb, on 4 October, according to UK maritime authorities. UKMTO logged the incident as suspicious activity, with the crew safe and no damage. No vessel name, flag or attacker details were given.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.

Corrections

Our 4 October brief reported a Turkish ship was attacked in the Black Sea, and an Albanian engineer aboard is reported missing; the described attack is the 27 September drone strike on the Palau-flagged bulker Aroyat near Novorossiysk, not an incident on 3 October.
