Wine Trader on fire after drone strike off Romania, seven missing

The San Marino-flagged tanker was hit about 13 nautical miles southeast of Sulina on 15 October; five of her 12 crew were rescued.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 19 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Romanian officials said a second tanker was also set on fire in the same area on 15 October.
  • Dumlupınar Gazetesi reported 12 dead and 11 missing but named no ships, dates or positions.
  • A headline reported a tugboat hit by projectiles off the UAE on 18 October, without naming the vessel.
  • Iran's Revolutionary Guards said an oil tanker hit a mine in the Strait of Hormuz on 19 October.

Romania's president said drones struck the San Marino-flagged tanker Wine Trader off Sulina on 15 October, and the Romanian naval authority reported the fire and seven of her 12 crew missing.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 4 in the Black Sea (3 the week before); 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (11 the week before); 1 in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden (1 the week before).

Black Sea

The vessel was about 13 nautical miles southeast of Sulina, one mile outside Romanian territorial waters, according to gCaptain and Al Jazeera. Romanian officials said a second tanker was also set on fire in the same area. The condition of the seven missing sailors was not given.

Dumlupınar Gazetesi reported successive attacks on Turkish ships in the Black Sea, with 12 dead and 11 missing. The report gave no vessel names, dates, positions or attribution.

Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran and Lancet drone crews struck a large dry cargo ship in the Black Sea on 18 October, including a vessel carrying Ukrainian military cargo west of Odesa. It gave no vessel name, flag, position or independent confirmation.

A headline citing The Hindu reported that 23 Indians were evacuated from a tanker near Sochi after an attack. It gave no vessel name, flag, date, damage or attribution details.

A headline reported escalating drone attacks against Black Sea shipping near NATO territory on 18 October. It gave no vessel name, flag, position or attribution details.

Earlier in the week, on 14 October, Romania's president confirmed drones struck the Comoros-flagged chemical tanker Yara J inside Romanian waters near Sulina; her crew were unharmed.

Earlier in the week, on 17 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a dry cargo ship carrying military and dual-use cargo about 40 km south of Odesa port, without naming the vessel.

Earlier in the week, on 15 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed strikes on dry cargo ships in the Bug Estuary and at Chornomorsk, with no names, flags or damage details. Reuters said it could not verify the claim.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

A headline reported that a tugboat was hit by projectiles off the UAE on 18 October. It gave no vessel name, flag, position or attribution details.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said an oil tanker caught fire after hitting a mine in the Strait of Hormuz on 19 October. The claim came from the Iranian side only, with no vessel name, flag or position given.

A headline reported an explosion on a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on 18 October amid continuing Saudi-Houthi clashes. It gave no vessel name, flag, position, damage or attribution details.

Earlier in the week, the IMO confirmed incidents list recorded GFS Giselle hit 6NM off the coast of Oman on 13 October, damaged with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, the IMO confirmed incidents list recorded Al Saadiyat hit 1 NM off the coast of Oman on 13 October, damaged with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, UKMTO warnings 174-26 and 175-26 recorded a tanker struck by an unknown projectile 68 nautical miles north-east of Al Jubayl and a second hit by an RPG from a small boat 78 nautical miles north-east, both on 16 October with all crew safe.

Earlier in the week, India's shipping minister Sarbananda Sonowal named the crude oil tanker MT ST 3, with 15 Indian seafarers aboard, struck by a projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 16 October, all safe.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Earlier in the week, UKMTO warning 176-26 recorded a skiff firing two shots at an LNG tanker 140 nautical miles south-west of Al Mukalla on 17 October, her armed team firing warning shots, no damage and no injuries.

Earlier in the week, a headline reported that an India-flagged ship sank after an attack in the Red Sea on 17 October, giving no vessel name, position, cause, casualty details or attribution.
