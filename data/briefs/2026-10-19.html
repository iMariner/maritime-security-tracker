Crew missing after drone strike sets Wine Trader on fire off Romania

The San Marino-flagged tanker was hit about 13 nautical miles southeast of Sulina on 15 October, with seven of her 12 crew missing.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 19 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Romania's president confirmed drones struck the Wine Trader, and the Romanian naval authority reported seven of her 12 crew missing.
  • UKMTO warning 177-26 said two unknown projectiles hit a tug northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October, causing one minor casualty.
  • Russia's defence ministry said its drones struck a large dry cargo ship west of Odesa on 18 October, without naming the vessel.

Romania's president confirmed that drones struck the San Marino-flagged tanker Wine Trader off Sulina on 15 October, and Romanian naval authorities reported the vessel on fire with seven of her 12 crew missing.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 4 in the Black Sea (3 the week before); 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (11 the week before); 1 in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden (1 the week before).

Black Sea

Five of the 12 crew were rescued. The vessel was about 13 nautical miles southeast of Sulina, one mile outside Romanian territorial waters, according to gCaptain and Al Jazeera. The Romanian naval authority reported the fire and the missing crew.

Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran and Lancet drone crews struck a large dry cargo ship in the Black Sea on 18 October, including a vessel carrying Ukrainian military cargo west of Odesa, and also destroyed an unmanned boat. No vessel name, flag, position or independent confirmation was given.

Earlier in the week, the Comoros-flagged chemical tanker Yara J was anchored about 10 nautical miles southeast of Sulina on the evening of 14 October when its crew reported an explosion and fire onboard, a suspected drone strike inside Romanian territorial waters.

Earlier in the week, russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a dry cargo ship carrying military and dual-use cargo about 40 km south of Odesa port on 17 October.

Earlier in the week, russia's defence ministry said its forces struck dry cargo ships carrying military and dual-use cargo, one in the Bug Estuary and one in the port of Chornomorsk.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO warning 177-26 reported a tug struck by two unknown projectiles northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October. The tug was damaged but continued her voyage, with one minor casualty.

Earlier in the week, the IMO lists the Gfs Giselle as hit near 6NM from the coast of Oman on 13 October.

Earlier in the week, the IMO lists the Al Saadiyat as hit near 1 NM from the coast of Oman on 13 October.

Earlier in the week, uKMTO reported that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile near Al Jubail, Saudi Arabia, on 17 October, causing a fire.

Earlier in the week, an Indian shipping official said a crude oil tanker was targeted by a missile in the Strait of Hormuz.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Earlier in the week, uKMTO reported that an LNG carrier was fired on by a small boat or skiff off the coast of Yemen on 17 October, with follow-up measures taken.
