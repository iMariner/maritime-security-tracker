Panama-flagged ship RITI catches fire near Iran's Lavan Island

Oman's navy evacuated 25 sailors from the vessel after the fire broke out about 40 nautical miles from the island, UKMTO said.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:40 UTC on 31 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Oman's navy evacuated 25 sailors from the RITI near Musandam after the fire.
  • UKMTO cited the vessel's Company Security Officer for the 29 October strike near Khasab.
  • A headline reported that oil prices climbed after a tanker attack in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Russia's defence ministry said it struck a dry cargo vessel carrying military cargo toward Odesa.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

The Panama-flagged ship RITI caught fire after being struck by an unknown projectile near Lavan Island, Iran, on 30 October, UKMTO said.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 4 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (5 the week before); 1 in the Black Sea (1 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO said the vessel was about 40 nautical miles from Lavan Island when the projectile struck. Oman's navy evacuated 25 sailors from the RITI near Musandam after the fire broke out. The extent of damage to the ship and her cargo was not immediately known.

A tanker was struck by an unknown projectile while completing an outbound transit of the Strait of Hormuz near Khasab, Oman, on 29 October at 1600 UTC, UKMTO said, citing the vessel's Company Security Officer. No crew injuries or environmental impact were reported.

A headline reported that oil prices climbed after a tanker attack in the Strait of Hormuz. No vessel name, flag, date, position, damage or casualty details were given.

A headline reported that a cargo ship reported a suspected attack while crossing the Strait of Hormuz on 30 October. No vessel name, flag, position, weapon type, damage or casualty details were given.

Earlier in the week, on 29 October, the Panama-flagged tanker LOUISE 1 caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after explosives were attached to its hull, and UKMTO reported the crew safe.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its forces struck a dry cargo vessel in the Black Sea carrying military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa on 30 October, as part of a massed overnight strike with drones and precision weapons. Russian outlets repeated the claim, which gave no vessel name, damage or casualties.

Earlier in the week, on 28 October, sea drones struck the Liberian-flagged container ship Contship Jet off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast en route to Constanța, Romania, and she sank; all 18 crew were rescued, according to the ship's owner and the Bulgarian government.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
