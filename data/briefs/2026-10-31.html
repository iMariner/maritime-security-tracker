Panama-flagged tanker RITI catches fire off Musandam, all 25 crew evacuated

Oman's Maritime Security Centre said its navy evacuated all 25 crew from the tanker after she was targeted and set on fire on 30 October.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:40 UTC on 31 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Oman's Maritime Security Centre said the Royal Oman Navy evacuated all 25 crew and gave them medical care.
  • UKMTO said the tanker was on outbound transit when struck, with no injuries reported.
  • Russia's defence ministry said the vessel carried military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

The Panama-flagged crude oil tanker RITI caught fire after being targeted off Musandam, Oman, on 30 October, and Oman's Maritime Security Centre said its navy evacuated all 25 crew.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 4 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (5 the week before); 1 in the Black Sea (1 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The RITI is a Panama-flagged crude oil tanker of 166,739 deadweight tonnes. Oman's Maritime Security Centre said the Royal Oman Navy evacuated all 25 crew and gave them medical care. UKMTO separately reported on 30 October that a vessel was hit by an unknown projectile 40 nautical miles south of Lavan Island, with crew safe and no pollution, and named no ship.

UKMTO reported, citing the vessel's Company Security Officer, that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile while completing an outbound transit of the Strait of Hormuz on 29 October at 1600 UTC. No crew injuries or environmental impact were reported.

Earlier in the week, a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz caught fire on 29 October after explosives were attached to its hull, and UKMTO reported the crew safe.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its forces struck a dry cargo vessel in the Black Sea carrying military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa during a massed overnight strike with drones and precision weapons on 30 October. The ministry gave no vessel name, damage or casualties.

Earlier in the week, sea drones struck the container ship Contship Jet off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast while she was en route to Constanța, Romania, on 28 October, according to the ship's owner and the Bulgarian government.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know
