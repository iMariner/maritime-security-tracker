Cargo ship Khoryv sinks off Romania after drone strike, one dead and three missing

The Comoros-flagged general cargo vessel went down about 13 nautical miles off Constanța on 23 October, Romanian authorities said.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 24 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Romanian officials blamed Russia for the drone strike that sank the Khoryv, and Azerbaijan coordinated consular support.
  • The UAE said Iranian missiles hit oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz on 23 October, killing one sailor.
  • The IMO lists JIF BARRACUDA as damaged off Khor Fakkan on 18 October, with one seafarer injured.
  • Turkish outlets reported a drone-hit tanker transited the Bosphorus, closing the strait to traffic.

A drone struck the general cargo ship Khoryv and sank it about 13 nautical miles off Constanța, Romania, on 23 October, killing one crew member and leaving three missing, Romanian authorities said.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 1 in the Black Sea (5 the week before); 6 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (11 the week before).

Black Sea

Nine crew were rescued after the Comoros-flagged vessel was hit, Romanian authorities said. Romanian officials blamed Russia for the attack, and Azerbaijan coordinated consular support afterward. The nationalities of those killed and missing have not been given.

A tanker that had been hit by a drone passed through the Istanbul Bosphorus, and the strait was closed to traffic, Turkish outlets reported. The reports gave no vessel name, date of the attack, damage or attribution.

TradeWinds reported that Russian drone strikes in the Black Sea are evading NATO defences, changing the threat to shipping. No specific vessel, position, damage or casualty details were given.

A Russian Telegram digest stated that a Romanian vessel was attacked in the Black Sea, alongside discussion of EU and NATO moves to tighten shipping controls. No vessel name, position, damage or casualty details were given.

Earlier in the week, Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran drones struck dry cargo ships and a container ship in the Black Sea between 21 and 23 October, saying they carried military cargo for Ukraine, with one vessel reported adrift off Giresun.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The UAE said Iranian missiles struck oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz on 23 October, killing one sailor, EnergyNow.com reported. No vessel names, flags or positions were given.

The IMO lists the Netherlands-flagged JIF BARRACUDA as damaged northeast of Khor Fakkan, UAE, on 18 October, with no pollution and one seafarer injured. The IRGC Navy said the tanker was hit by a sea mine.

Earlier in the week, on 21 October, the Kuwaiti tanker AL DASMA was struck by an unknown projectile about 11 nautical miles off Bukha, Oman, starting a fire that was extinguished with all crew safe, UKMTO reported.

Earlier in the week, on 21 October, the Liberian tanker SOYIA was struck by an unidentified projectile in the northern Persian Gulf southeast of Iraq's Al Faw port, with all crew safe, UKMTO reported.

Earlier in the week, on 19 October, the IMO listed the tanker CHLOE (MIVERVA V) as damaged 121 nautical miles from the coast of Oman, with no pollution.

Earlier in the week, on 22 October, UKMTO received a time-late report that a tanker was hit by an unknown projectile 7 nautical miles northeast of Limah, Oman, and was able to continue its voyage.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Earlier in the week, on 22 October, reports said an Omani ship was targeted off Zuqar Island while reportedly transporting weapons to the Houthis, with no vessel name, damage, casualties or attribution given.
