Bulk carrier Cape Dao sinks in Strait of Hormuz after strike

Lloyd's List reported the bulk carrier sank after a projectile strike on 26 October; UKMTO said a vessel was on fire off Iraq the same day.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:40 UTC on 27 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Lloyd's List reported the Cape Dao sank after the 26 October strike in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Romanian authorities said the Khoryv sank off Constanța on 23 October, with one dead and three missing.
  • TradeWinds News reported a bulk carrier sank off Oman, without naming the vessel.
  • The Times of India headline said Iran hit a US-linked ship near an allied Arab nation on 27 October.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

The bulk carrier Cape Dao sank in the Strait of Hormuz after a projectile strike on 26 October, Lloyd's List reported, while UKMTO said a tanker was disabled in the strait.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 3 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (4 the week before); 1 in the Black Sea (2 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO reported the projectile strike that disabled a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on 26 October. Lloyd's List reported that the bulk carrier Cape Dao sank after the attack. UKMTO also reported a vessel on fire off Iraq's Al-Faw coast in the Persian Gulf the same day, giving no name, cause or casualties.

TradeWinds News reported in a headline that a bulk carrier sank off Oman a month after an Iranian attack. The item gave no vessel name, flag, position, damage details or casualties.

The Times of India reported in a headline on 27 October that Iran hit a US-linked ship near an allied Arab nation, with the crew's fate unknown. The item gave no vessel name, flag, position or damage details.

The Times of India reported in a headline that Iran fired cruise missiles at vessels in the Strait of Hormuz on 26 October and that explosions rocked the area. No neutral source confirmed the claim, and no vessel name, flag, damage or casualty details were given.

Black Sea

Romanian authorities said the Comoros flagged general cargo ship Khoryv was struck by a drone and sank about 13 nautical miles off Constanța on 23 October, with one crew member killed, nine rescued and three missing. Romanian officials blamed Russia.

Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck dry cargo vessels bound for Odesa and Chornomorsk on 26 October, saying they carried military equipment for Ukraine. It also claimed strikes on port infrastructure and warehouses at Chornomorsk, and gave no vessel names, flags, damage or casualties.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
