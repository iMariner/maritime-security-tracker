Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah hit in Strait of Hormuz

Vanguard Tech names the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah as the latest tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz, the second strike on a Kuwait Oil Tanker Company VLCC in four days.

Key points

  • Vanguard Tech identified the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah as the latest tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • UKMTO confirmed a tanker caught fire after an attack in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October.
  • A projectile attack at Saudi Arabia's Yanbu port briefly disrupted loading operations, shipping media reported.
  • Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel bound for Odesa.
  • Ukraine's Defense Forces claimed they struck a vessel along with an oil facility and other Russian targets.

Maritime security firm Vanguard Tech identified the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah as the latest tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz, according to a report carried by shipping media. It is the second strike on a Kuwait Oil Tanker Company VLCC in four days, Vanguard Tech said. The crew were reported safe and a fire broke out on board.

The incident is the most serious of a run of attacks on tankers in and around the Strait of Hormuz, with UKMTO confirming a separate tanker fire on 1 October. In the Black Sea, Russia and Ukraine each claimed strikes on vessels.

Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO confirmed that a tanker caught fire after an attack in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, the first such attack in October. No vessel name, flag or responsibility was given. UKMTO also released four late reports of attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz from 28 and 29 September.

Vanguard Tech named the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah as the latest casualty in the Strait of Hormuz, the second strike on a Kuwait Oil Tanker Company VLCC in four days, according to the report. The crew were reported safe and a fire broke out on board.

A separate projectile attack at Saudi Arabia's Yanbu port briefly disrupted loading operations, according to Splash247. No vessel was named and no responsibility was stated. Saudi Aramco and Saudi port authorities have not confirmed or attributed the incident.

UKMTO confirmed that three vessels were reported struck on Tuesday, 29 September, in the area around the Strait of Hormuz. No vessel names, flags or responsibility were given.

A Turkish-language report raised the question of whether a missile attack was carried out on a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz, but no independent or official source confirms it. No vessel name, flag, date or responsibility was given.

A Chinese-owned oil tanker was reported hit near the Strait of Hormuz, according to EnergyNow.com. No vessel name, flag or responsibility was given.

As tensions escalated in the Strait of Hormuz, both the US and Iran fired at ships, according to ABC News. No vessel names, flags or further details were given.

A maritime agency reported that two tankers were hit by unknown projectiles in the Strait of Hormuz. No vessel names, flags or responsibility were given.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry said its drones struck a general cargo vessel at sea in the Black Sea that was carrying military and dual-use cargo to Odesa port. The claim was not independently confirmed and no vessel name was given.

Ukraine's Defense Forces said they struck a vessel along with an oil facility and other Russian targets. No vessel name, flag or type was given.

What this means for crews

Crews transiting the Strait of Hormuz and the Black Sea should follow the latest UKMTO and JMIC guidance and their company security procedures. Report any suspicious approach or attack to UKMTO without delay. Keep AIS and bridge watch procedures in line with the ship security plan.
