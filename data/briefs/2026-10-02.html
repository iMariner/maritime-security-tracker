Kuwait-flagged tanker KAZIMAH III hit in Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO reported a tanker struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz, with the crew safe. Russia claimed drone strikes on cargo vessels in the Black Sea.

Key points

  • UKMTO reported the Kuwait-flagged tanker KAZIMAH III was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz, crew safe.
  • Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel en route to Odesa and a vessel at Chornomorsk.
  • Ukraine's President Zelenskiy said Ukrainian forces struck a Russian-linked vessel in the Black Sea.
  • UKMTO confirmed three vessels were struck in the Strait of Hormuz on 29 September.

UKMTO reported that the Kuwait-flagged tanker KAZIMAH III was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, starting a fire, with the crew safe.

The incident is the latest in a series of attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and the Black Sea. Russia and Ukraine both claimed strikes on vessels in the Black Sea, while UKMTO confirmed multiple vessels were hit in the Strait of Hormuz earlier in the week.

Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO reported that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, starting a fire. The crew were reported safe. Shipping media and security firm Vanguard Tech named the vessel as the Kuwait-flagged VLCC KAZIMAH III, owned by Kuwait Oil Tanker Co.

UKMTO confirmed that three vessels were reported struck on 29 September in the Strait of Hormuz. Other reports described four vessels hit in 24 hours and a Chinese-owned oil tanker hit near Hormuz. A cargo ship also reported a suspected attack while crossing, and the US and Iran both fired at ships amid escalating tensions.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel in the Black Sea that was carrying military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa. It separately said it hit a vessel used for military cargo at Chornomorsk port. The claims have not been independently confirmed.

Ukrainian forces struck a Russian-linked vessel in the Black Sea along with other targets, according to President Zelenskiy and RBC-Ukraine. No vessel name, type or damage details were given.

What this means for crews

Crews in the Strait of Hormuz and the Black Sea should follow UKMTO and JMIC guidance, report any suspicious activity to UKMTO, and adhere to company security procedures. Vessels should maintain heightened vigilance and report all incidents promptly.
