Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah III hit in Strait of Hormuz

Kazimah III struck by unknown projectile on 1 October, crew safe. Russia claims Black Sea cargo ship strike. No new Red Sea attacks.

Key points

  • A tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, starting a fire, with the crew reported safe by UKMTO.
  • Shipping media and security firm Vanguard Tech named the vessel as the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah III.
  • A Panama-flagged tanker was reported hit with a fire on board in the Strait of Hormuz on 2 October.
  • Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel heading to Odesa on 2 October.
  • President Zelenskiy claimed Ukrainian forces struck a Russian-linked vessel in the Black Sea on 1 October.

A tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, starting a fire, with the crew reported safe, according to UKMTO.

The Strait of Hormuz saw the most serious incidents of the past 24 hours, while the Black Sea remained tense with competing claims from Russia and Ukraine. No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO Warning 147-26 reported that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, causing a fire. The crew were reported safe. Shipping media and security firm Vanguard Tech named the vessel as the Kuwait Oil Tanker Company VLCC Kazimah III.

A Panama-flagged tanker was reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz on 2 October with a fire on board. Maritime security firm Ambrey reported the tanker was struck by a projectile about 5.3 nautical miles north-northwest of Kumzar and reviewed imagery of smoke from the stern. UKMTO has published no matching warning naming this vessel.

The IMO confirmed attacks on three vessels in the Strait of Hormuz in late September. SINBAD (IMO 9413688) was hit on 29 September, MERSIN PROSPERITY (IMO 9327554) was hit on 29 September, and AL FUNTAS (IMO 9653408) was hit on 28 September. All three were damaged with no pollution reported.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel in the Black Sea on 2 October that was carrying military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa. The ministry separately said it hit a vessel used for military cargo at Chornomorsk port. The claims have not been independently confirmed.

Ukrainian forces struck a Russian-linked vessel in the Black Sea on 1 October along with other targets, according to President Zelenskiy and RBC-Ukraine. No vessel name, type or damage details were given. Only Ukraine's president and Ukrainian outlets reported the strike, and no neutral or Russian confirmation was found.

What this means for crews

Vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz and the Black Sea should follow UKMTO and JMIC guidance and report any suspicious activity to UKMTO immediately. Ship managers should review company security procedures and ensure crews are briefed on the latest threat picture. In the event of an attack, crews should follow emergency drills and report to UKMTO without delay.
