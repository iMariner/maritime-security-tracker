Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah reported hit in Strait of Hormuz

Vanguard Tech names the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah as the latest tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz, the second Kuwait Oil Tanker Company VLCC struck in four days.

Key points

  • ●Vanguard Tech reported the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah was hit in the Strait of Hormuz, the second Kuwait Oil Tanker Company VLCC struck in four days.
  • ●UKMTO confirmed a tanker caught fire after an attack in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, with the crew reported safe.
  • ●A projectile attack at Saudi Arabia's Yanbu port briefly disrupted loading operations, according to shipping media.
  • ●Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel bound for Odesa with military and dual-use cargo.
  • ●Ukraine's Defense Forces claimed they struck a vessel along with an oil facility and other Russian targets.

Maritime security firm Vanguard Tech identified the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah as the latest tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz, the second strike on a Kuwait Oil Tanker Company VLCC in four days.

The reported attack on the Kazimah came as UKMTO confirmed a tanker fire in the Strait of Hormuz and shipping media reported a projectile strike at Saudi Arabia's Yanbu port. In the Black Sea, Russia and Ukraine each claimed attacks on vessels.

Strait of Hormuz

Vanguard Tech reported that the Kuwait-flagged VLCC Kazimah was struck in the Strait of Hormuz, with a fire on board and the crew reported safe. No responsibility was stated for the attack, which was reported rather than confirmed by a neutral authority.

UKMTO confirmed that a tanker caught fire after an attack in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, the first such attack in October. The crew were reported safe, and no vessel name, flag or responsibility was given.

UKMTO also released four late reports of attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz from 28 and 29 September. No vessel names, flags or responsibility were given in those reports.

Shipping media reported a projectile attack at Saudi Arabia's Yanbu port that briefly disrupted loading operations. No vessel was named and no responsibility was stated. Saudi Aramco and Saudi port authorities have not confirmed or attributed the incident.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel at sea in the Black Sea that it said was carrying military and dual-use cargo to Odesa port. The claim was not independently confirmed and no vessel name was given.

Ukraine's Defense Forces claimed they struck a vessel along with an oil facility and other Russian targets. No vessel name, flag or type was given, and the claim was not independently confirmed.

What this means for crews

Vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz and the Black Sea should follow current UKMTO and JMIC guidance and report any suspicious approach or attack to UKMTO without delay.

Ship managers should review company security procedures and ensure crews know the reporting channels before entering these areas.
