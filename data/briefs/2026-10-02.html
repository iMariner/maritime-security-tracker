Kuwait-flagged VLCC KAZIMAH III hit in Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO confirms a Kuwait-flagged VLCC was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, as Russia claims drone strikes in the Black Sea.

Key points

  • UKMTO reported the Kuwait-flagged VLCC KAZIMAH III was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, crew safe.
  • A Panama-flagged tanker was reported hit with a fire on board in the Strait of Hormuz on 2 October, according to Ambrey.
  • Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel heading to Odesa and a vessel at Chornomorsk port.
  • President Zelenskiy claimed Ukrainian forces struck a Russian-linked vessel in the Black Sea on 1 October.
  • The IMO confirmed AL FUNTAS, MERSIN PROSPERITY and SINBAD were damaged in the Strait of Hormuz on 28 and 29 September.

UKMTO reported that the Kuwait-flagged VLCC KAZIMAH III was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, starting a fire, with the crew reported safe.

The attack on the Kuwait-flagged tanker was the most serious incident of the day in the Strait of Hormuz. In the Black Sea, Russia and Ukraine both claimed strikes on vessels, neither independently confirmed. No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.

Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO Warning 147-26 reported that a tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 1 October, starting a fire, with the crew safe. Security firm Vanguard Tech and shipping media named the vessel as the Kuwait-flagged VLCC KAZIMAH III, operated by Kuwait Oil Tanker Company.

Maritime security firm Ambrey reported that a Panama-flagged tanker was struck by a projectile about 5.3 nautical miles north-northwest of Kumzar on 2 October, with a fire on board. Ambrey said it reviewed imagery of smoke from the stern. UKMTO has published no matching warning naming this vessel.

The IMO confirmed that AL FUNTAS was damaged in the Strait of Hormuz on 28 September, with no pollution reported. UKMTO released late reports of attacks on 28 and 29 September.

The IMO also confirmed that MERSIN PROSPERITY was damaged in the Strait of Hormuz on 29 September, with no pollution reported. The IMO confirmed that SINBAD was damaged in the Strait of Hormuz on 29 September, with no pollution reported.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a general cargo vessel in the Black Sea that was carrying military and dual-use cargo toward Odesa. The ministry separately claimed it hit a vessel used for military cargo at Chornomorsk port. The claims have not been independently confirmed.

President Zelenskiy and Ukrainian outlets said Ukrainian forces struck a Russian-linked vessel in the Black Sea on 1 October, along with other targets. No vessel name, type or damage details were given, and no neutral or Russian confirmation of the strike was found.

What this means for crews

Crews in the Strait of Hormuz and the Black Sea should follow UKMTO and JMIC guidance, and report any incident to UKMTO without delay. Ship managers should review company security procedures and ensure vessels maintain strict reporting with naval authorities. Vessels should remain vigilant and avoid loitering in high-risk areas.
