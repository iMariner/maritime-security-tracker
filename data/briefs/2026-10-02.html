Strait of Hormuz

UKMTO confirmed that three vessels were struck in the Strait of Hormuz on 29 September. The vessels included a Kuwait-flagged tanker, according to UKMTO.

Other reports described four vessels hit in 24 hours in the Strait of Hormuz. A Chinese-owned oil tanker was reported hit near Hormuz.

A cargo ship reported a suspected attack while crossing the Strait of Hormuz. The US and Iran both fired at ships amid escalating tensions.

What this means for crews

Follow UKMTO and JMIC guidance for the Strait of Hormuz. Report any suspicious activity to UKMTO. Apply your company security procedures.
