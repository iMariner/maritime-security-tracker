Oil-bulk-ore carrier Khoryv sinks off Romania, one dead and three missing

The Comoros-flagged ship went down about 13 nautical miles off Constanța on 23 October; nine crew were rescued.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:40 UTC on 26 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • Romania's interior ministry said nine crew were rescued from the Khoryv after the strike set the ship ablaze.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.

The oil-bulk-ore carrier Khoryv sank about 13 nautical miles off Constanța, Romania, on 23 October after a drone strike, killing one crew member and leaving three missing, Romanian authorities said.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 1 in the Black Sea (4 the week before); 2 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (7 the week before).

Black Sea

Romania's interior ministry said the drone strike caused major damage and a fire on the Comoros-flagged ship. The Romanian Naval Authority is leading the search for the three missing, and nine crew were rescued, the ministry said. Romanian officials blamed Russia, while Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones hit two cargo vessels in the same overnight attack without naming the second ship.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

Earlier in the week, on 24 and 25 October, Iran said two unauthorised oil tankers were struck in the Strait of Hormuz, a claim that is Iran's alone, while other outlets reported explosions and exchanges of fire in the same waters without vessel names or confirmed damage.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden in the last 24 hours.
