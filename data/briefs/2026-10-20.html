Tug hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The tug was hit northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October, with one minor casualty reported by UKMTO.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 06:06 UTC on 20 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • UKMTO warning 177-26 reported the tug was hit northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October.
  • UKMTO reported an LNG tanker was hit near Yemen on 19 October, causing a fire.
  • UKMTO reported a tanker was attacked with a rocket-propelled grenade off Saudi Arabia on 19 October.
  • Russia's defence ministry said drone strikes hit three dry cargo vessels unloading in Reni port on 20 October.

UKMTO reported that a tugboat was struck by two unknown projectiles northeast of Khor Fakkan off the UAE on 18 October, causing one minor casualty.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 3 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (13 the week before); 1 in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden (1 the week before); 2 in the Black Sea (3 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO warning 177-26 reported the tug was hit by two projectiles northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October, with the position approximate. The warning reported one minor casualty. The tug's name and flag were not given.

UKMTO reported that a tanker was attacked with a rocket-propelled grenade off Saudi Arabia on 19 October. A separate headline reported three tankers damaged in the Gulf as the US-Iran conflict escalated, covering the same Gulf attacks.

A Turkish headline reported that a huge oil tanker that had been attacked by a UAV passed through the Strait of Hormuz. No vessel name, flag, date, position, damage or attribution details were given.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy said a non-compliant tanker was hit by a sea mine south of the Strait of Hormuz. The claim comes from the Iranian side only; no vessel name, flag, position or damage details were given.

A Guardian headline said people are scouring the Strait of Hormuz for spilt cargo after Iran attacked container ships. No vessel names, flags, dates or positions were given.

Earlier in the week, on 16 October, an Indian shipping official said the crude oil tanker ST 3 was targeted by a missile in the Strait of Hormuz, with all crew safe and no pollution reported.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

UKMTO reported that an LNG tanker was hit near Yemen on 19 October, causing a fire. A roundup headline separately reported an LNG carrier taking fire in the Gulf of Aden, describing the same strike. No vessel name or flag was given.

A headline reported that drone strikes stalled two fuel tankers bound for Kenya. No vessel names, flags, dates, positions, damage details or attribution were given, and the region was not stated.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry said overnight drone strikes hit three dry cargo vessels unloading military and dual-use cargo in Reni port, Odesa region. The claim comes from the Russian side only; no vessel names, flags or damage details were given.

Russia's defence ministry said a dry cargo vessel carrying military cargo to Ukraine was struck at sea while heading to Odesa port. The claim comes from the Russian side only; no vessel name, flag or position was given.

Russia's defence ministry claimed a dry cargo ship carrying military and dual-use cargo was struck at sea 130 km southwest of Odesa port during the night of 19 October. No vessel name, flag, damage or casualty details were given, and the claim was not independently confirmed.

A headline reported escalating tensions in the Black Sea following drone strikes on commercial vessels. No vessel name, flag, date, position, damage or attribution details were given.

Earlier in the week, on 18 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran and Lancet drone crews struck a large dry cargo ship in the Black Sea, including a vessel carrying Ukrainian military cargo west of Odesa, and also destroyed an unmanned boat. No vessel name, flag, position or independent confirmation was given.

Earlier in the week, on 17 October, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck a dry cargo ship carrying military and dual-use cargo about 40 km south of Odesa port. The same Russian claim said strikes hit Ukrainian steel plants and a military cargo ship, with no vessel name, flag or damage details given.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
