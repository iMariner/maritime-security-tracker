Tanker Aviva hit by drone in Izmail port, Odesa region

A Russian Geran-4 jet drone struck the Turkish tanker Aviva in Izmail, according to the Telegram channel Dva Mayora, with no damage or casualty details given.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 9 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • The Turkish tanker Aviva was struck by a drone in Izmail port, Odesa region, according to Dva Mayora.
  • No damage or casualty details were given for the Aviva attack.
  • TASS reported Russian forces struck an unnamed cargo ship bound for Chornomorsk.
  • Syria said nine of its nationals are missing after a drone sank a ship in the Black Sea.
  • The Aframax tanker Rio was left ablaze in the Black Sea, its crew unharmed, Russian officials said.

The Turkish tanker Aviva was struck by a Russian Geran-4 Sicker jet drone in the port of Izmail, Odesa region, on 8 October, according to the Telegram channel Dva Mayora.

The attack is one of four reported in the Black Sea in the last 24 hours, the iMariners tracker shows, which records four merchant ships hit there in the last seven days compared with none in the seven days before.

Black Sea

No damage or casualty details were given for the Aviva, and the report did not say whether the vessel was loaded or at berth. The claim comes from Dva Mayora, a Telegram channel, and has not been confirmed by Ukrainian or Turkish authorities.

TASS reported that Russian forces struck a cargo ship at sea that was delivering supplies to the Ukrainian port of Chornomorsk. The report gives no vessel name, flag, position or damage details, and the claim comes from the Russian Ministry of Defence via TASS.

Syria said nine of its nationals are missing after a drone attack sank a ship in the Black Sea. The report, carried by ETV Bharat and Elnashra and citing the Syrian Ports Authority, gives no vessel name, flag or exact position.

The Aframax tanker Rio was attacked in the Black Sea and left ablaze, with an oil slick reported off the Russian coast. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said the crew was unharmed and that consequences were limited.

Earlier in the week, a Turkish vessel was hit by drones in Romania's exclusive economic zone and sank completely, according to a Romanian news report, which gave no vessel name or casualty details.

Strait of Hormuz

A Panama-flagged crude oil tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on 8 October, according to a UK maritime agency, with reports of 11 Indian crew injured. The UN halted its escort of ships through Hormuz after the vessel came under attack, and a Times of India headline separately said Iran attacked a ship in UAE waters. The attacker and the exact position are not yet known.

What crews should know

The iMariners tracker records six merchant ships reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in the last seven days, the same as in the seven days before, and one in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden compared with none previously. Today's attacks happened in Izmail port in the Odesa region and at sea bound for Chornomorsk, both in the Black Sea, and in the Strait of Hormuz. Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.

Corrections

Our 8 October brief reported a fuel tanker ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz after a drone strike, according to Iran's Revolutionary Guards; the EnergyNow item is a republished March 2026 report about the IRGC claim on the Honduran-flagged Athe Nova, not an attack on 7 October.

Our 8 October brief reported that an agricultural news outlet said ships carrying rapeseed oil and grain in the Black Sea were targeted; it names no ship, flag or position and repeats the 5 and 6 October Black Sea attacks already tracked.
