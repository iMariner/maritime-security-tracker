Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO warning 183-26 recorded the fire from multiple third party reports. The vessel's name, flag, cause of the fire, damage details and crew status remain unknown.

Iran's army said an unnamed oil tanker struck naval mines and caught fire in a prohibited area east of the Strait of Hormuz on 27 October, IRNA reported. No ship, flag, crew or casualty details were given and there is no independent confirmation.

Earlier in the week, a projectile strike disabled a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on 26 October, and Lloyd's List reported the bulk carrier Cape Dao sank after the attack.

Black Sea
