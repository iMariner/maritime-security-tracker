Tanker hit by projectiles off Qatar, casualties reported

UKMTO reported a tanker struck about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal at about 1900 UTC on 7 October, with casualties.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 07:47 UTC on 8 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • A tanker was struck by multiple projectiles off Qatar on 7 October, with casualties reported, UKMTO said.
  • The vessel has not been named and no group has claimed the attack.
  • Shots were fired at the Singapore-flagged containership CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea on 7 October.
  • Bulgaria called off the search for the Alfa Watan's crew, ten or twelve Syrian seafarers missing and feared dead.
  • The Houthis claimed a Saudi tanker was targeted off Yanbu on 6 October, with no damage reported.

A tanker was struck by multiple projectiles about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal, Qatar, at about 1900 UTC on 7 October, with casualties reported, UKMTO said.

It is the sixth merchant ship reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in seven days, up from five in the previous seven, according to the iMariners tracker. The vessel has not been named and no group has claimed responsibility.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO reported the attack on the tanker in the Arabian Gulf on 7 October, saying casualties were reported. The agency gave no vessel name, flag or further details, and no group has claimed responsibility. What caused the projectiles and the extent of the damage remain unknown.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Shots were fired at the Singapore-flagged containership CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea early on 7 October, according to The Maritime Executive and Lloyd's List. The vessel is operated by Chinese CUL Lines, and the crew were unharmed, the reports said. No group has claimed responsibility and the circumstances remain unclear.

Earlier in the week, the Houthis claimed they targeted a Saudi oil vessel off Yanbu on 6 October, with no details on damage or casualties.

Black Sea

Bulgaria called off the search on 7 October for the crew of the Alfa Watan, which sank on 6 October after Russian strikes on four Turkish ships, according to TradeWinds. Ten or twelve Syrian seafarers are missing and feared dead, with Maritime.bg reporting ten aboard and Bulgaria's rescue chief saying twelve. No individual vessel names were given for the other ships.

What crews should know

The day's incidents were the tanker hit off Qatar and the shots at the CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea. Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
