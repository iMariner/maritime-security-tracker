Turkish cargo ship hit by drone off Bulgaria, 10 crew reported killed

NationalTurk says the general cargo vessel was struck off Bulgaria in the Black Sea, with ten crew killed; no attacker named.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:38 UTC on 8 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • A Turkish general cargo ship was hit by a drone off Bulgaria, with 10 crew reported killed, per NationalTurk.
  • The vessel is not named and no exact position or attacker was given.
  • A Cyprus-linked tanker is ablaze off Sochi after a sea drone attack, Cyprus Mail reported.
  • A fuel tanker is ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz after a drone strike, Iran's Revolutionary Guards said.
  • Shots were fired at a Chinese containership in the southern Red Sea early on Wednesday, The Maritime Executive reported.

A Turkish general cargo ship was attacked by a drone off Bulgaria in the Black Sea on 7 October, with ten crew members killed, according to NationalTurk.

The Black Sea has seen six merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, up from none in the previous seven, the iMariners tracker shows. The Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf has seen five in each period.

Black Sea

The Turkish vessel was struck off Bulgaria on 7 October, NationalTurk reported. The report gave no vessel name, exact position or attacker, and the ten deaths are its claim alone.

A Cyprus-linked tanker was ablaze off Sochi on 7 October after a sea drone attack, according to Cyprus Mail, which reported the strike as Ukrainian. Russia's environmental watchdog Rosprirodnadzor was cited. No vessel name or casualty details were given.

Bulgaria stopped the search for missing seafarers after strikes on four Turkish ships, TradeWinds reported. One sank and others were damaged in the 6 October attack off Bulgaria, which the outlet attributed to Russia. No individual vessel names were given.

The Palau-flagged general cargo ship Able was damaged in a drone attack off Bulgaria on 6 October and its crew was rescued, Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed. The same attack, about 70 nautical miles east of Byala, sank the vessel Alfa Watan, with the two ships about five nautical miles apart.

An agricultural outlet reported that ships carrying rapeseed oil and grain in the Black Sea were targeted on 7 October, without names, flags, positions or casualties. An expert on Fox News said a Russian Geran-4 drone was likely used in a Black Sea ship attack, without giving a vessel name, flag, position or casualties.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

A fuel tanker was ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz on 7 October after a drone strike, Iran's Revolutionary Guards said. No vessel name, flag or casualty details were given.

TradeWinds reported that a Maran Gas LNG carrier was hit near the Strait of Hormuz on 7 October, in a series of attacks it attributed to Iran. No damage or casualty details were given.

Red Sea

Shots were fired at a Chinese containership in the southern Red Sea early on Wednesday, 7 October, The Maritime Executive reported. Details were unclear and no group claimed responsibility.

What crews should know

In the Black Sea, six merchant ships have been reported hit in the last seven days, against none in the seven days before, while the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf has seen five in each period, according to the iMariners tracker.

Today's attacks happened off Bulgaria, off Sochi, in the Strait of Hormuz and in the southern Red Sea.
