Tanker hit by projectiles off Qatar, casualties reported

A tanker was struck about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal, with casualties, UKMTO said.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 05:08 UTC on 8 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • A tanker was hit by multiple projectiles off Qatar on 7 October, with casualties, UKMTO said.
  • The strike happened about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal at about 1900 UTC.
  • Shots were fired at the Singapore-flagged container ship CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea on 7 October.
  • Ten or twelve crew of the Alfa Watan are missing and feared dead after the 6 October sinking.
  • No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in the last 24 hours.

A tanker was struck by multiple projectiles about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal, Qatar, at about 1900 UTC on 7 October, with casualties, UKMTO said.

It is the fifth merchant ship reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in seven days, according to the iMariners tracker, the same count as the seven days before.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO reported the tanker hit by multiple projectiles north of Madinat ash Shamal on 7 October, with casualties. The agency did not name the vessel, and no group has claimed the attack. The number of casualties and the extent of damage were not immediately known.

Oman's On Peace was evacuated earlier, and that incident is separate from the Qatar strike.

No new attacks on merchant ships were reported in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in the last 24 hours.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Shots were fired at the Singapore-flagged container ship CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea early on 7 October, according to The Maritime Executive and VesselFinder. The vessel is operated by Chinese CUL Lines. The crew was unharmed, and there was no claim of responsibility.

Black Sea

Ten or twelve crew of the general cargo ship Alfa Watan are missing and feared dead after it sank off Bulgaria on 6 October, with the number aboard disputed. Maritime.bg said ten, while Bulgaria's rescue chief said twelve, all Syrian. Bulgaria called off the search on 7 October with no trace of the sailors.

The Palau-flagged general cargo ship Able was damaged in the same drone attack off Bulgaria on 6 October, and its crew was rescued, Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed. The two ships were about 5 nautical miles apart, about 70 nautical miles east of Byala, inside Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone, according to Prime Minister Radev.

What crews should know

In the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf, five merchant ships were reported hit in the last seven days, the same as the seven days before, according to the iMariners tracker. In the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden, one ship was reported hit in the last seven days, against none in the seven days before, while the Black Sea saw five, against none in the seven days before.

The day's incidents were the tanker hit off Qatar and the shots at the CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea.

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
