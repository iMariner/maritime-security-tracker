Tanker hit by projectiles off Qatar with casualties aboard

UKMTO reported the strike about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal at about 1900 UTC on 7 October.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 08:17 UTC on 8 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • A tanker was struck by multiple projectiles off Qatar on 7 October, with casualties reported.
  • UKMTO gave the position as about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal.
  • Shots were fired at the Singapore-flagged containership CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea on 7 October.
  • The Gulf has seen six merchant ships reported hit in seven days, the iMariners tracker says.
  • Ten or twelve crew of the Alfa Watan are missing and feared dead after the 6 October sinking.

A tanker was struck by multiple projectiles about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal, Qatar, at about 1900 UTC on 7 October, with casualties reported, UKMTO said.

The strike is the sixth merchant ship reported hit in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf in seven days, against five in the previous seven, according to the iMariners tracker. The vessel has not been named and no group has claimed the attack.

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

UKMTO reported the tanker hit by multiple projectiles in the Arabian Gulf on 7 October, with casualties. The agency gave no vessel name, flag or further details, and the attacker and cause remain unknown.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Shots were fired at the Singapore-flagged containership CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea early on 7 October, according to The Maritime Executive. The ship is operated by Chinese CUL Lines, the outlet said. No one has claimed responsibility and the crew were unharmed, Lloyd's List and The Maritime Executive reported.

Earlier in the week, the Houthis claimed they targeted a Saudi oil vessel off Yanbu on 6 October, with no details on damage or casualties.

Black Sea

Bulgaria stopped the search on 7 October for seafarers missing after Russian strikes on four Turkish ships, according to TradeWinds. Ten or twelve crew of the general cargo ship Alfa Watan are missing and feared dead, with Maritime.bg reporting ten aboard and Bulgaria's rescue chief saying twelve, all Syrian. The vessel sank on 6 October.

What crews should know

The day's incidents were the tanker hit off Qatar and the shots at the CUL Klang in the southern Red Sea. Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
