Iran claims seven tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz, unconfirmed

The IRGC said four vessels transferring oil ship to ship were struck at once, but UKMTO has issued no warning matching the claim.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 23 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • The IRGC's seven-vessel claim is unconfirmed and UKMTO has issued no warning matching it.
  • The IMO lists the Netherlands-flagged tug JIF BARRACUDA as damaged off Khor Fakkan on 18 October.
  • Russia's defence ministry said its Geran drones struck four ships in the Black Sea on 22 October.
  • Reports of an Omani ship targeted off Zuqar Island gave no name, damage or attribution.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed on 22 October that seven vessels were attacked or struck mines across the Persian Gulf in 24 hours, a claim UKMTO has not matched with any warning.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 4 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (13 the week before); 0 in the Black Sea (5 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The IRGC gave no vessel names, flags or positions for the seven vessels it says were attacked or struck mines, and the claim is unconfirmed. President Trump accused Iran of faking the hits to push up oil prices, gCaptain reported on 22 October. UKMTO has issued no warning matching the claim.

The Netherlands-flagged multi-purpose tug JIF BARRACUDA was damaged northeast of Khor Fakkan in the UAE on 18 October, with no pollution and one seafarer injured, according to the IMO confirmed incidents list. Her operator Jifmar and VesselFinder give her type as a tug.

Earlier in the week, on 21 October, the Kuwaiti-flagged tanker AL DASMA was struck by an unknown projectile about 11 nautical miles off Bukha, Oman, causing a fire that was put out with all crew safe, UKMTO reported.

Earlier in the week, on 20 October, the Liberian-flagged tanker SOYIA was struck by an unidentified projectile 30 nautical miles southeast of Iraq's Al Faw port, with all crew safe, UKMTO reported.

Earlier in the week, on 19 October, the tanker CHLOE (MIVERVA V) was damaged 121 nautical miles from the coast of Oman, with no pollution, according to the IMO confirmed incidents list.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran drones struck three dry cargo ships and a container ship in the Black Sea on 22 October carrying military cargo. It gave no names, and there is no independent confirmation.

The Palau-flagged general cargo ship Able, damaged by a drone off Bulgaria on 6 October, later sank off the Turkish coast after her crew was taken off, Sofia Globe reported on 20 October.

Earlier in the week, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck dry cargo ships in the Black Sea south and west of Odesa across 17 to 19 October, without names or independent confirmation.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

Reports said an Omani ship was targeted off Zuqar Island while reportedly carrying weapons to the Houthis. No name, position, damage, casualties or attribution were given, and the claim is unconfirmed.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
