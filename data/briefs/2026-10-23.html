IMO confirms tanker JIF BARRACUDA damaged off Khor Fakkan

The Netherlands-flagged tanker was hit northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October, injuring one seafarer, according to the IMO.

Reporting period: the 24 hours to 04:39 UTC on 23 October 2026. By the iMariners Security Desk.

Key points

  • The IMO lists the tanker as damaged with one seafarer injured and no pollution.
  • Russia's defence ministry said its Geran drones struck dry cargo and container ships in the Black Sea on 22 October.
  • The IRGC claimed seven vessels were attacked or struck mines in the Persian Gulf in 24 hours.
  • A Yemeni army threat to attack an Indian ship in the Red Sea was reported, with no strike confirmed.

The IMO has confirmed that the Netherlands-flagged tanker JIF BARRACUDA was damaged northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October, with one seafarer injured and no pollution reported.

Merchant ships reported hit in the last seven days, according to the iMariners tracker: 4 in the Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf (13 the week before); 0 in the Black Sea (5 the week before).

Strait of Hormuz and the Gulf

The IMO's confirmed incidents list records the tanker JIF BARRACUDA as hit northeast of Khor Fakkan, UAE, on 18 October. The vessel was damaged with no pollution and one seafarer injured. Iran's IRGC Navy said the tanker was hit by a sea mine. UKMTO warning 177-26 reports a tug struck by two projectiles northeast of Khor Fakkan on 18 October.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed that seven vessels were attacked or struck mines across the Persian Gulf over the past 24 hours, including four conducting ship-to-ship oil transfers that were struck simultaneously. The claim comes from the IRGC only; no vessel names, flags or positions were given.

Earlier in the week, on 21 October, the Kuwaiti tanker AL DASMA was struck by an unknown projectile about 11 nautical miles off Bukha, Oman, causing a fire that was extinguished with all crew safe, according to UKMTO.

Earlier in the week, on 20 October, the Liberian-flagged tanker SOYIA was struck by an unidentified projectile in the northern Persian Gulf southeast of Iraq's Al Faw port, with all crew safe, according to UKMTO.

Earlier in the week, on 19 October, the tanker CHLOE (MIVERVA V) was damaged 121 nautical miles from the coast of Oman, with no pollution reported, according to the IMO.

Black Sea

Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran drones struck several dry cargo ships and a container ship in the Black Sea on 22 October, saying they carried military cargo, with one vessel reported adrift and no names or damage given. Turkish reports said an attacked cargo ship drifted off Giresun with a Turkish crew member aboard, and RIA Novosti said two vessels bound for Odesa and Chornomorsk were hit.

A Turkish headline reports growing concern over attacks on ships in the Black Sea. No vessel names, dates, positions, damage or attribution are given.

The Palau-flagged general cargo ship ABLE was damaged in a drone attack off Bulgaria in the Black Sea on 6 October, and its crew was rescued, Bulgaria's prime minister and government confirmed. The same attack sank the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan, with the two ships about 5 nautical miles apart.

Earlier in the week, Russia's defence ministry claimed its Geran drones struck dry cargo vessels in the Black Sea heading to Odesa and Chornomorsk on 21 October, saying they carried military cargo for Ukraine, with no vessel names, flags, positions, damage or casualties given.

Earlier in the week, Russia's defence ministry claimed its drones struck dry cargo ships carrying military and dual-use cargo in the Black Sea south and west of Odesa across 17 to 19 October, with no vessel names, flags, positions or independent confirmation given.

Red Sea and Gulf of Aden

A headline says the Yemeni army threatened to attack an Indian ship in the Red Sea and international shipping. No vessel name, position, date of any actual attack or damage was given; this is a threat, not a reported strike.

Reports say an Omani ship was targeted off Zuqar Island while it was reportedly transporting weapons to the Houthis. No vessel name, exact position, damage, casualties or attribution are given, and the claim is unconfirmed.

What crews should know

Report any attack or suspicious approach to UKMTO, and follow UKMTO and JMIC advisories and company security instructions.
