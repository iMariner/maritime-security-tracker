# Historical backfill, second pass: 2 November 2026

Adds 75 reviewed vessel/date records to the living tracker (276 to 351 public records). This is still a reviewed record, not a complete war count, so the page keeps its backfill notice.

## Method

1. Five research passes, one per region and period, listed named merchant-ship attacks missing from the published feed. Each record needed at least two opened sources, at least one authoritative (IMO, UKMTO, JMIC, CENTCOM, governments, owners, or Reuters/AP through maritime press). Wikipedia and aggregator lists were used only to discover incidents.
2. Three independent fact-check passes re-opened every cited source and returned ok, fix or drop for each record. Result: 58 ok, 17 corrected, 3 dropped.
3. Records were merged into `data/living-history.json` and `data/living-evidence.json` only after `tracker.living.verification` accepted them, with a duplicate check (same vessel name or IMO within two days) against every published record.

## Left out on purpose

- No attack date in any source (only an announcement date): LORD NELSON, SMARTA, OCEAN KOI, LOUISE 1 and BANDA (Black Sea, July 2026).
- Identification too weak or contradicted: KHALISSA, MAERSK YORKTOWN (9 April 2024, owner said it was not the target), NCC GHAZAL, DAISY, HAJI ALI.
- Russian state claims only, one called likely false by trade press: SGV-FLOT, SORMOVSKIY 3064, APACHE.
- Earlier holds still unresolved: KATHLEEN, PRP-52 (floating pier), BLACK FUEL, AJ ROSE. VOLGONEFT-25 (27 September 2024) is now resolved and published.

## Remaining gaps

Web search limits cut every research pass short. Known gaps: named ships hit in the July to December 2023 strikes on Odesa, Izmail and Reni; December 2023 Red Sea days missing from the CENTCOM mirror; US strikes on 8 September 2026 (DERYA, KAVIZ, CHARMINAR, RIESCO) awaiting a second source; strong single-source leads SELAT STORM, TARIF, DISHA, YASA POLARIS, AIDA, SEA DESTINY and DANIA J. These belong to the Hermes living-evidence desk or a later backfill pass.
